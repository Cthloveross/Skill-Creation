## How Top-5 is selected

Top-5 is a model tool action, not a hard-coded slice and not the token-sampling `top_k` value:

1. BM25 returns up to ten headers per `search_docs` call; bodies are withheld.
2. The runner accumulates the ordered union of IDs exposed by successful searches.
3. Qwen calls `select_docs(resource_ids)` once with exactly five unique IDs.
4. The runner rejects the call unless all five IDs were previously exposed. A valid selection is
   immutable; subsequent search is rejected.
5. `read_doc` is then limited to selected IDs and at most five unique bodies. Selecting five does
   not require reading all five.
