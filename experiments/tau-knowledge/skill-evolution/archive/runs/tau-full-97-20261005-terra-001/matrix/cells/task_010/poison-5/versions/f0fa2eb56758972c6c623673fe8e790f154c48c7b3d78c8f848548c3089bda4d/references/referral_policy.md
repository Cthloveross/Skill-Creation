# Documented credit-card referral policy reference

## Program-wide rules

- A customer can receive at most two referral bonuses in any rolling seven-day window. The cap applies across all credit-card types.
- The third and any subsequent referrals in that rolling window are automatically denied. A referral denied for the limit cannot be reinstated within that same seven-day window.
- The rolling window is based on the timing of successful referral bonuses, rather than calendar weeks.
- Referral requirements and bonus amounts vary by card. A customer should have an active offer for the specific card before a referral link is created.

## Statuses

- `COMPLETE`: the referred person successfully opened a new account and met the criteria for the referral bonus. The bonus is granted under the applicable program terms.
- `IN_PROGRESS`: the referred person successfully opened a new account and is working toward the criteria for the referral bonus.
- `NO_PROGRESS`: the referred person has not applied.
- `APPLIED`: the application is awaiting a decision.
- `REJECTED`: the user has too many referral processes going on. Do not retry immediately; review existing referral activity before advising next steps.
- `ERROR`: an error occurred; retry later or escalate internally if it persists.

Customers can track referral status through the mobile app or customer service.

## Card-specific documented terms

### Silver Rewards Card

The referred person must be approved and spend at least $750 within 60 days of account opening. The referral bonus normally posts one to two billing cycles after the referred account meets that requirement. The program states an annual maximum of seven referral bonuses.

### Platinum Rewards Card

The referred person must be approved and spend at least $1,500 within 90 days of account opening. Bonuses are credited after approval and timely qualifying spend. Self-referrals and duplicate applications do not qualify. The program states an annual maximum of seven referral bonuses.

No bonus amount, qualifying-spend rule, or payout schedule should be inferred for another card type from these Silver or Platinum terms.
