# Packaged Savings Product Requirements

Use these facts only when the customer's exact selected `account_class` matches. Product specifications may change; if an account is not listed, obtain current authoritative product documentation before opening or funding it.

| Official savings account class | Minimum opening deposit | Other documented opening requirement | Notes |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Bronze Account | $0 | None documented | 2.0% APY; no monthly maintenance fee. |
| Green Account | $100 | Paperless statements required | Ongoing documented minimum balance is $500; up to 8 free withdrawals monthly. |
| Gold Plus Account | $10,000 | None documented | Ongoing documented minimum balance is $25,000; up to 25 withdrawals monthly. |

A generic savings-opening procedure permits the customer to defer funding, in which case they must fund through internal transfer or external deposit within 30 days or the new account will close. When the customer funds immediately, validate that the transfer is positive, source funds are sufficient, and the applicable documented minimum is met.

## APY-pairing reference

Only the following checking/savings pairings have a documented linked-checking APY boost: Green checking + Gold savings; Green Fee-Free + Bronze; Evergreen + Green; Blue + Silver Plus; Light Green + Diamond Elite; Dark Green + Bronze; Bluest + Silver; Purple + Platinum Plus; Gold Years + Gold Plus; Light Green + Platinum; Green checking + Silver; Evergreen + Diamond Elite; Bluest + Bronze; Purple + Gold; Gold Years + Silver; Blue + Platinum; Green Fee-Free + Gold Plus; Dark Green + Platinum Plus.

This list identifies eligibility only; it does not provide boost percentages. Do not promise a percentage absent the applicable product documentation.
