Personal checking opening policy
===============================

A personal checking account requires all of the following:
- The customer is verified.
- The customer is at least 18 years old.
- The customer has fewer than four personal checking accounts before an additional account is opened.
- The customer has no checking account closed for cause in the prior six months.
- The customer confirms the desired account_class using its full official name.
- The account is then opened with open_bank_account_4821.

Customer-account retrieval
==========================

get_all_user_accounts_by_user_id_3847 retrieves a customer's checking and savings accounts and includes account_id, account_type, account_class, status, balance, and date_opened. The retrieved response must be evaluated for the opening eligibility checks. If it does not establish closure-for-cause history, that prerequisite remains unconfirmed.

Overdraft-related product facts
===============================

Green Account (checking)
- Transactions that would push the available balance negative are declined at authorization.
- It does not perform overdraft-protection transfers from linked accounts.
- Its overdraft-protection transfer fee is $0.00.
- Real-time authorization may consider a buffer up to $5 for minor final-amount differences; a transaction exceeding the balance by more than that threshold is declined.

Blue Account
- Account overdraft fee: $0.00.
- Optional overdraft-protection transfer: $12.50 per transfer.

Light Blue Account
- Monthly maintenance fee: $0.00.
- Overdraft fee: $0.00.
- Overdraft-protection transfer fee: $0.00.

Green Fee-Free Account
- Overdraft fee: $0.00.
- Monthly maintenance fee: $22.50.
- The monthly fee is waived when the minimum daily balance is at least $150.

Identity verification runtime requirement
=========================================

The verification logging tool requires successful confirmation of two out of four customer fields: date of birth, email, phone number, and address. After confirmation, retrieve the current timestamp and log a verification record with the customer record's required fields. A customer name alone is not two-factor identity confirmation.
