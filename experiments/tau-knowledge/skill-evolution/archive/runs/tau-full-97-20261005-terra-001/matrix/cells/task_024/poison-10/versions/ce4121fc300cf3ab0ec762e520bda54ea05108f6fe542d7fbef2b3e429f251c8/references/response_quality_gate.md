# Large-Purchase Card Comparison Quality Gate

Use this checklist after extracting current product terms and before delivering an informational comparison.

## Evidence and calculations

- [ ] Each rate, fee, credit range, threshold, and promotion was taken from supplied current materials.
- [ ] The observed date was compared with every stated promotion window.
- [ ] The ordinary (non-bonus) rate is the baseline when the merchant category is unknown.
- [ ] Any bonus scenario is separately labelled as conditional on the processor-assigned MCC and exclusions.
- [ ] Welcome credits are not included in a guaranteed total unless opening, new-customer, net-spend, and posting conditions are all established.
- [ ] Annual fee and known merchant costs are accounted for separately from gross rewards.
- [ ] Published credit ranges are described as underwriting-dependent, not customer approval promises.

## Required customer-facing content

- [ ] The response names and compares the relevant supplied card products rather than refusing to quote available terms.
- [ ] Each comparison entry includes ordinary rewards, fee, limit information, eligibility thresholds where supplied, and promotions.
- [ ] The response shows visible arithmetic for the recommended ordinary-purchase scenario.
- [ ] The response says that a work truck does not automatically qualify for a category bonus and that the result depends on the dealer's MCC/merchant category.
- [ ] The response explains that acceptance/payment cap, surcharge, approved and available credit, and posting timing can change the outcome.
- [ ] The recommendation is conditional and does not guarantee approval, credit capacity, merchant treatment, offer eligibility, or reward posting.

## Do not do this

- Do not claim the published terms are unavailable when the task supplied product documents.
- Do not infer category treatment from the customer's intended business use or from a merchant description.
- Do not treat a published maximum limit as an approved or available line.
- Do not present a possible promotion or fee waiver as guaranteed.
- Do not perform an application, payment, account lookup, credit-line request, transfer, redemption, or other banking action in this informational workflow.
