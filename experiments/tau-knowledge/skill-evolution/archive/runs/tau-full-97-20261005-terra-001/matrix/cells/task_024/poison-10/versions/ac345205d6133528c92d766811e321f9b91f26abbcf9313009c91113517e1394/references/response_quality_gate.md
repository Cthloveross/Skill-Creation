# Large-Purchase Card Comparison Quality Gate

Use this checklist after reviewing supplied product documents and before responding.

## Evidence and computation

- [ ] Product-specific source material supports every stated reward rate, fee, limit, threshold, and offer.
- [ ] The task's observed date was checked against each promotion window.
- [ ] The ordinary/non-bonus rate is the baseline if dealer MCC is unknown.
- [ ] Each bonus result is separately described as dependent on processor-assigned MCC and exclusions.
- [ ] A welcome credit is conditional on opening date, new-customer eligibility, threshold, net purchases, and posting requirement.
- [ ] Known annual fees and dealer charges are shown separately from gross rewards.
- [ ] Unknown surcharge is not silently assumed to be zero.
- [ ] A published maximum limit is not represented as approved or available credit.

## Customer-facing obligations

- [ ] Answer the current card-options request; do not claim supplied terms are unavailable.
- [ ] Name Business Bronze, Business Silver, Business Gold, and Business Platinum when their documents are supplied.
- [ ] Include ordinary reward rate, MCC-conditioned bonus rate, fee, line range, material eligibility, and promotion for each relevant product.
- [ ] State a direct conditional recommendation and show the ordinary-rate arithmetic.
- [ ] For a Bronze promotion scenario, state the opening dates, qualifying net-spend threshold, and that purchases must post within the first two months.
- [ ] State that the work truck does **not automatically** earn a category bonus and that this **depends on** dealer MCC / merchant category.
- [ ] Explain the effects of card acceptance, payment cap, split tender, surcharge, actual approved/available credit, and posting timing.
- [ ] State that returns and credits can reduce rewards and qualifying spend where source terms provide that rule.
- [ ] If carrying a balance is possible, identify supplied APR and caution that interest can outweigh rewards.

## Do not do this

- Do not replace sourced comparison with an unsupported referral or refusal.
- Do not infer category treatment from business purpose, truck type, or dealer marketing.
- Do not call a promotion, fee waiver, approved line, or reward posting guaranteed.
- Do not perform a banking action in this informational workflow.
