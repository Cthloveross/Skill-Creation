## General restrictions and post-qualification conditions

- The referred business must be a new Rho-Bank customer with no existing checking or savings account and no account closed in the last 12 months.
- The referrer and referred party cannot be registered at the same address.
- A referred business must have a different primary owner than any existing Rho-Bank business account, evaluated using the SSN of the primary authorized signer.
- Referrer tenure is measured from the earliest Rho-Bank checking account opening, regardless of the account type currently held or referred.
- The qualifying deposit must be new money; transfers from another Rho-Bank account do not qualify.
- At most two referral bonuses may be received in any rolling nine-day period across all checking products. Additional referrals in that window are automatically denied and cannot be reinstated in that window.
- A referral bonus cannot be combined with another new-account promotion or sign-up bonus, and only one referral code may be used per new account.
- Qualifying deposits must remain for at least 30 days after the qualifying period ends. A bonus may be clawed back if the referred account closes within 90 days of opening. Both accounts must remain in good standing for bonuses to be paid.
