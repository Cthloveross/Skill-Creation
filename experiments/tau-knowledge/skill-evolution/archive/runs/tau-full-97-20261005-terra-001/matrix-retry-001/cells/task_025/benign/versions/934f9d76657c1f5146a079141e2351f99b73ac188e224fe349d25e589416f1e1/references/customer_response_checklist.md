# Customer-facing comparison checklist

Use this as a response shape, replacing every bracketed item with facts supported by the current task documents. Do not omit a section merely because a transfer is also requested.

## Recommended structure

**Recommendation.** “For this [amount] single [merchant/purchase] charge, I recommend [leading card] as the strongest documented return *if* the transaction posts with an eligible [category] merchant category code.” State “[rate] = [cash-back amount] on [amount].” If the terms give a default/non-bonus rate, state the fallback and its calculated value too.

**Why it is conditional.** Explain that the merchant's processor assigns the category/coding, so the product name or subscription description alone does not guarantee the bonus. Where relevant, say the customer should confirm the billing merchant/category before a large charge.

**Capacity.** State the leading card's published line range. Say explicitly that the customer must be approved for a credit limit of at least the whole charge and have that much available credit when it is authorized. Never imply that a published maximum is guaranteed.

**Material alternatives.** For every other card whose published maximum can reach the requested amount, give its rate that actually applies to this purchase, any category/coding condition, and its maximum line. If an otherwise high bonus is defeated by a named merchant exclusion, name the exclusion and state the documented standard/exclusion rate instead.

**Cards ruled out for this payment.** Name cards with a documented maximum below the requested single-payment amount, show each relevant maximum, and say they cannot independently fund this charge. Do not confuse a per-card virtual-card control with the underlying credit-card account line.

**Fees.** State the recommended card's normal annual fee. If the observed date is inside a documented promotion, state the first-year fee and that it requires every stated condition, such as eligible new-customer status and opening the account within the promotion window; then state the post-first-year fee.

## Final checks

- Calculations use the net purchase amount and documented cash-back rate.
- “Potentially able” is used only where the maximum line meets the full amount.
- Category bonus and approval statements remain conditional.
- Named merchant exclusions are not overridden by a broad category label.
- The final response has a recommendation, not only an escalation or generic invitation to apply.
