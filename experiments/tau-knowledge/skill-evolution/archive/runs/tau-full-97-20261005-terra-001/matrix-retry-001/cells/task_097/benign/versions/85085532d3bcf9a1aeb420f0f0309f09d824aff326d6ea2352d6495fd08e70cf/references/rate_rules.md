# Covered savings APY rules

Percentages are annual percentage points. Add only documented, verified components to the base or tier APY before deriving a daily periodic rate. Verify that the rates and qualifications applied during the statement period being reviewed.

## Silver Account

- 2.5% for a daily ending balance below $10,000; 4.0% at or above $10,000.
- Interest compounds daily and is credited monthly.
- Card bonuses: Bronze Rewards 0%; Silver Rewards 0.1%; Gold Rewards 0.5%; Platinum Rewards 0.2%; Diamond Elite Card 0.25%; EcoCard 2.2%; Green Rewards 0%; Crypto-Cash Back 0.5%.
- Checking pairing: Bluest checking with Silver savings, +0.45%.
- A 0.025% relationship bonus may apply when its criteria are met.

## Silver Plus Account

- 3.0% below $15,000; 4.5% at or above $15,000.
- Verified active direct deposit adds 0.25% for the eligible active period.
- Interest compounds daily and is credited monthly.
- Card bonuses: Bronze Rewards 0.15%; Silver Rewards 0.15%; Gold Rewards 0.2%; Platinum Rewards 0.15%; Diamond Elite Card 0.4%; EcoCard 0.45%; Green Rewards 0.1%; Crypto-Cash Back 0%.
- Checking pairing: Blue checking with Silver Plus savings, +0.35%.
- A 0.025% relationship bonus may apply when its criteria are met.

## Platinum Account

- Base APY: 6.5%; interest compounds daily and is credited monthly.
- Card bonuses: Bronze Rewards 0%; Silver Rewards 0%; Gold Rewards 0.15%; Platinum Rewards 0.25%; Diamond Elite Card 0.35%; EcoCard 0%; Green Rewards 0%; Crypto-Cash Back 0%.
- Checking pairings: Blue checking with Platinum savings, +0.8%; Light Green checking with Platinum savings, +0.65%.

## Diamond Elite Account

- Base APY: 7.5%; interest compounds daily and is credited monthly.
- Card bonuses: Bronze Rewards 0%; Silver Rewards 0%; Gold Rewards 0%; Platinum Rewards 0.1%; Diamond Elite Card 0.5%; EcoCard 0%; Green Rewards 0%; Crypto-Cash Back 0.15%.
- Checking pairings: Light Green checking with Diamond Elite savings, +0.2%; Evergreen checking with Diamond Elite savings, +0.15%.

## Selection and eligibility

Use only the highest applicable active same-profile card bonus and only the highest applicable qualifying checking boost. Bonuses do not stack within either category. The selected card bonus and checking boost may stack with the base or tier rate and a separately verified relationship bonus.

A checking percentage applies only to its exact named pairing when the accounts are linked under the same customer profile, open, in good standing, and otherwise eligible. An account list showing that a customer owns both products is not proof of the required pairing.

Light Green is available only while its primary holder is age 13 through 24. Do not include its Platinum or Diamond Elite percentage in an expected APY if verified age information creates an unresolved eligibility conflict.
