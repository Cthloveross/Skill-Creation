# Documented product facts for plan construction

Use these facts only after checking they remain current in the knowledge available to the executor. Values below are percentage points where marked APY.

## Replacement and closure rules

- A personal checking opening requires verified identity, customer age 18+, no more than four personal checking accounts, and no checking account closed for cause in the last six months. The official account class must end in `Account`.
- A personal savings opening requires verified identity, an active Rho-Bank checking account held at least 14 days, fewer than five personal savings accounts, no collections, and no negative account balance. The official class must end in `Account`.
- Personal checking closure requires OPEN status, no pending transactions, and either a zero balance or enough balance to pay an applicable early closure fee. Light Blue is entry tier: $15 if closed within 30 days.
- A new savings account that is not immediately internally funded has a 30-day funding window, after which it will be closed.

## Relevant checking feature

| Checking account | Documented relevant feature |
|---|---|
| Blue Account | Supports automatic overdraft-protection transfers from a linked eligible account; each protection-triggered transfer costs $12.50. |
| Green Account (checking) | Does not perform overdraft-protection transfers. |

Blue Account linking/enrollment is customer-controlled in account settings and requires acceptance of the $12.50 per-transfer disclosure.

## Card and transfer cautions

- EcoCard application requires standard identity and income information and is subject to approval. No agent card-application action is documented in this package.
- Internal transfers require ACTIVE or OPEN source and destination accounts, sufficient source funds, same customer ownership, distinct valid IDs, positive USD amount, and customer authorization.
