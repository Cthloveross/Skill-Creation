# Offer and routing evidence

## Documented offer comparison

The available documented promotion with a $10,000 net-purchase requirement in the first two months is the **Business Bronze Rewards Card** new-customer offer. It provides a **$500 statement credit** after qualification. The related application documentation describes business application information. This does not establish a $1,000 offer for a personal card.

## External promotion routing

The human-agent reason-code guidance defines `unconfirmed_external_communication` as the Tier 2 route when a customer claims a specific promotion, program, or offer based on a letter, email, or flyer and the agent cannot verify or find it after searching the knowledge base.

The same guidance says selection is tiered: choose the highest applicable reason. General customer frustration and generic human requests are lower-tier reasons. An allegation of false advertising alone is not a subpoena, court order, garnishment, or other legal/regulatory matter.

## Account-credit constraint

The statement-credit process requires a customer `user_id`, a credit-card account ID, a positive credit amount, and a permitted reason. It is appropriate only after a credit is warranted. An unverified pre-application flyer does not establish entitlement and, without an account, lacks the required target account.
