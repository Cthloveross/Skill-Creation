# Documented credit-card referral policy

Use these terms only for the named cards. A referral card not listed here has no card-specific referral amount, spend requirement, or payout timing confirmed by this reference.

## Status meanings

- **COMPLETE**: The referred person successfully opened a new account and met the criteria for the referral bonus. The bonus is granted under the applicable program terms.
- **IN_PROGRESS**: The referred person successfully opened a new account and is working toward the bonus criteria. No action is required; monitor it until the criteria are met.
- **NO_PROGRESS**: The referred person has not applied. The referrer may remind them to start an application.
- **APPLIED**: The referred person applied and awaits a decision. No manual intervention is needed.
- **REJECTED**: Too many referral processes are underway. Do not retry immediately; review existing referral activity before advising next steps.
- **ERROR**: An error occurred. Retry later or escalate internally if it persists.

## Cross-card rolling weekly limit

A customer can receive at most two referral bonuses in any rolling seven-day window across all credit card types. A third or subsequent referral in that interval is automatically denied and cannot be reinstated within the same seven-day window. The calculation is based on exact timestamps rather than calendar weeks.

## Card-specific programs

### Silver Rewards Card

- Bonus: 75 for each successful referral.
- Requirement: the referred person must be approved and spend at least $750 within 60 days of account opening.
- Annual limit: up to seven referral bonuses per calendar year.
- Payout: normally one to two billing cycles after the referred account meets the spend requirement.

### Platinum Rewards Card

- Bonus: $100 for each friend who is approved and meets the qualifying spend.
- Requirement: at least $1,500 in spending within 90 days of account opening.
- Annual limit: up to seven referral bonuses per calendar year.
- Payout: credited after approval and the spend requirement are met.
- Self-referrals and duplicate applications do not qualify.

### EcoCard

- Bonus: 50 for each successful referral.
- Requirement: the referred person must be approved and spend at least $500 within 60 days of account opening.
- Annual limit: up to seven referral bonuses per calendar year.
- Payout: normally one to two billing cycles after the referred account meets the spend requirement.
