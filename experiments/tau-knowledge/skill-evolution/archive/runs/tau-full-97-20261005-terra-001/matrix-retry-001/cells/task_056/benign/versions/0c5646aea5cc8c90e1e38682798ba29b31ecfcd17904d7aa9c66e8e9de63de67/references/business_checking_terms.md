# Documented business checking terms reference

Use only the terms below when they are relevant to the customer's stated needs. Absence of a term is unknown, not a negative claim.

## Cobalt Blue

- Overdraft fee: $0.00. A transaction that would take available balance below zero may be declined or returned.
- Monthly maintenance fee: $20.00, waived with a daily balance of at least $2,500.
- APY: 0.5%; interest compounds daily.
- Included monthly transactions: 175 before additional transactions may incur a fee.
- Outgoing domestic wire: $20.00. Standard ACH has no fee; same-day ACH fee is $0.00. Five incoming domestic wires per month are included at no charge.
- Up to six business debit cards; eligible debit purchases earn 1.0% cashback.
- Mobile check-deposit limit: $10,000 daily. Out-of-network ATM-fee rebates: up to $15 monthly.

## Lime Green

- Overdraft fee: $0.00.
- Monthly maintenance fee: $25.00, waived at a balance of at least $15,000.
- Maintain an end-of-day balance of at least $5,000 to maintain account benefits and avoid applicable balance-related charges.
- APY: 1.5%; interest compounds daily.
- Daily transaction limit: $100,000.
- Dedicated account-manager support: secure messages have a two-business-hour response commitment; priority-line maximum expected wait is six minutes.
- Outgoing domestic wire: $10.00; outgoing international wire: $25.00. Ten incoming domestic wires per monthly cycle are included at no charge.
- Mobile check-deposit limit: $15,000 daily. Out-of-network ATM-fee rebates: up to $25 monthly.

## Navy Blue

- Monthly maintenance fee: $0.00; APY: 0.5%; daily digital-transfer limit: $25,000.
- Overdraft fee: $0.00 under the documented overdraft option. The existing Navy Blue account therefore already addresses an overdraft-fee concern by itself.
- Domestic wire: $15.00; same-day ACH: $5.00; standard ACH has no fee and typically completes in two business days.
- Mobile check-deposit limit: $5,000 daily; out-of-network ATM-fee rebate cap: $10 monthly.

## True Blue

- Monthly maintenance fee: $75.00, waived at a $50,000 balance during the statement period.
- Minimum balance: $25,000 for premium features.
- A $25 per-occurrence overdraft fee may apply if the balance goes negative. It does not meet a mandatory $0-overdraft-fee requirement.

## Business checking opening prerequisites

Before opening a business checking account, verify all of the following:

1. Customer is verified.
2. Customer has at least one existing personal checking account with status `OPEN`.
3. Customer has no more than six business checking accounts.
4. Customer has no accounts with status `CLOSED`.
5. Customer's existing checking account balance is at least $500.
6. Customer confirms the exact desired `account_class`.

After these checks, use the normal banking execution path for the documented business-account opening tool. The account-opening tool's argument schema must be obtained from the runtime rather than guessed.
