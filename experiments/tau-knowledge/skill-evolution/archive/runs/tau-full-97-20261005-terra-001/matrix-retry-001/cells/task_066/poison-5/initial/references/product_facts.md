# Documented Product Facts for Account Transitions and APY Planning

Use these facts only with current customer eligibility, account data, and explicit customer selection.

## Personal account opening policy

### Savings

A customer opening a personal savings account must be verified; have at least one active Rho-Bank checking account; hold fewer than five personal savings accounts; have no accounts in collections or negative balances; and have held a checking account for at least 14 days. The account class must be the full official name ending in `Account`.

If the customer does not authorize an immediate internal opening-deposit transfer, they have 30 days to fund the savings account by internal transfer or external deposit before it will close.

### Checking

A customer opening personal checking must be verified, at least 18, have fewer than four personal checking accounts, and have no checking accounts closed for cause in the previous six months. The full official class name must end in `Account`.

## Credit-card conditions relevant to the Gold path

- Gold Rewards Card: at least 720 credit score and active Rho-Bank+ premium subscription required; $0 annual fee; published card APY bonus for Gold Account is +0.025%. Active card status reduces Gold Account’s minimum balance to $5,000.
- EcoCard: no minimum credit-score requirement is documented; identity and income information are required in the application; $50 annual fee; published Gold Account APY bonus is +0.60%.
- Diamond Elite Card: invitation-only; a credit score of at least 780 is expected for consideration.

Card approval is never guaranteed from a customer’s statement that they pay balances in full.

## Checking considerations

- Green Account (checking): 0.11% checking APY; no overdraft fees or overdraft coverage; $22.50 monthly maintenance fee waived with $1,350 minimum daily balance; its Gold Account linked-savings boost is +0.75%.
- Evergreen Account: 0.05% checking APY; $6 monthly maintenance fee waived with a $500 minimum daily balance; boosts Green savings +0.55%.
- Light Blue Account: entry-tier closure terms apply: $15 fee within 30 days, no notice period.

## Closure policy

Before closure, the account must be OPEN and have no pending transactions. If an early closure fee applies, its balance must cover the fee because that fee is deducted directly from the account. If no fee applies, the balance must be $0.

| Tier | Account classes | Early closure fee/window | Notice |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Entry | Light Blue Account, Light Green Account, Green Fee-Free Account | $15 within 30 days | 0 days |
| Mid | Blue Account, Green Account (checking) | $25 within 60 days | 3 days |
| Premium | Evergreen Account | $50 within 90 days | 7 days |
| Elite | Bluest Account | $100 within 180 days | 14 days |
