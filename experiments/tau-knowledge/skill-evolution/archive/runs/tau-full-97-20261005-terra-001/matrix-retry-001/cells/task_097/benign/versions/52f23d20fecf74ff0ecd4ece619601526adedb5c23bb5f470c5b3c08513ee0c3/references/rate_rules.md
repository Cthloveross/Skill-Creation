# Covered savings APY rules

Percentages are annual percentage points. Add only supported, verified components to the base/tier APY before converting to a daily periodic rate. Confirm rates and qualifications for the relevant statement period.

## Silver Account

- Base tier: 2.5% when the daily ending balance is below $10,000.
- Higher tier: 4.0% when the daily ending balance is at least $10,000.
- Interest is compounded daily and credited monthly.
- Card bonuses: Bronze Rewards 0%; Silver Rewards 0.1%; Gold Rewards 0.5%; Platinum Rewards 0.2%; Diamond Elite Card 0.25%; EcoCard 2.2%; Green Rewards 0%; Crypto-Cash Back 0.5%.
- Documented checking pairing: Bluest checking + Silver savings, 0.45%.
- A 0.025% relationship bonus may apply when relationship criteria are met.

## Silver Plus Account

- Tier 1: 3.0% below $15,000.
- Tier 2: 4.5% at or above $15,000.
- Direct deposit adds 0.25% for a period in which it is active.
- Card bonuses: Bronze Rewards 0.15%; Silver Rewards 0.15%; Gold Rewards 0.2%; Platinum Rewards 0.15%; Diamond Elite Card 0.4%; EcoCard 0.45%; Green Rewards 0.1%; Crypto-Cash Back 0%.
- Documented checking pairing: Blue checking + Silver Plus savings, 0.35%.
- A 0.025% relationship bonus may apply when relationship criteria are met.
- Interest is compounded daily and credited monthly.

## Platinum Account

- Base APY: 6.5%.
- Card bonuses: Bronze Rewards 0%; Silver Rewards 0%; Gold Rewards 0.15%; Platinum Rewards 0.25%; Diamond Elite Card 0.35%; EcoCard 0%; Green Rewards 0%; Crypto-Cash Back 0%.
- Documented checking pairings: Blue checking + Platinum savings, 0.8%; Light Green checking + Platinum savings, 0.65%.
- Interest is compounded daily and credited monthly.

## Diamond Elite Account

- Base APY: 7.5%.
- Card bonuses: Bronze Rewards 0%; Silver Rewards 0%; Gold Rewards 0%; Platinum Rewards 0.1%; Diamond Elite Card 0.5%; EcoCard 0%; Green Rewards 0%; Crypto-Cash Back 0.15%.
- Documented checking pairings: Light Green checking + Diamond Elite savings, 0.2%; Evergreen checking + Diamond Elite savings, 0.15%.
- Interest is compounded daily and credited monthly.

## Selection, linkage, and eligibility policy

Only the highest applicable active card bonus is used. Only the highest applicable boost from active, qualifying, linked checking accounts is used. The selected card and checking components can stack with base/tier APY and a separately verified relationship bonus.

A documented checking percentage applies only to its exact named checking-to-savings pairing when the accounts are linked under the same customer profile, open, and in good standing. Ownership of both accounts, including their appearance in one account-list result, is not evidence of that link.

Light Green checking is available only to a primary holder who remains age 13 through 24. Therefore, a Light Green percentage must not be included in an expected rate if verified date-of-birth/current-date evidence creates an unresolved age-eligibility conflict.

Do not assume a rate component that is not documented here or not evidenced in the case.
