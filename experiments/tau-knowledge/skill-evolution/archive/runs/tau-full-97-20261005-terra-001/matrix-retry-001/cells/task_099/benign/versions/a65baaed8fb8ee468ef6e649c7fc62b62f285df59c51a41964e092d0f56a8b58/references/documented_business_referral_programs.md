## General conditions

- The referred business must be new to Rho-Bank, cannot share the referrer's registered address, and must have a different primary owner from any existing Rho-Bank business account.
- Qualifying funds must be external new money, not another Rho-Bank transfer.
- Qualifying funds must remain in the account for at least 30 days after the qualifying period ends.
- At most two successful referral bonuses may be received in any rolling nine-day period across checking products.
- The referral bonus cannot be combined with another new-account promotion or sign-up bonus, and only one referral code/offer may be used per new account.
- Both accounts must remain in good standing. A bonus may be clawed back if the referred account closes within 90 days of opening.

World Blue referral bonuses are typically credited within 7–10 business days after qualification. The World Blue referred business receives a separate $200 welcome bonus, which is not part of the referrer's bonus comparison.
