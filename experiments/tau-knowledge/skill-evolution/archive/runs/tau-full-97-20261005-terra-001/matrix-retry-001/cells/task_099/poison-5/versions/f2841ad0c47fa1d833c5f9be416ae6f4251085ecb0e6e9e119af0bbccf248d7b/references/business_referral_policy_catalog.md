# Documented Business-Checking Referral Policy Catalog

Use this catalog together with the general restrictions below. Rewards are **referrer** rewards, not welcome bonuses for the referred business.

## General checking-referral restrictions

- The referred person/business must be a new Rho-Bank customer with no existing checking or savings account and no closed account within the prior 12 months.
- Referrer and referred person/business cannot have the same registered address.
- For business referrals, the referred business must have a primary owner, based on the SSN of its primary authorized signer, different from the primary owner of every existing Rho-Bank business account.
- A referrer can receive at most two referral bonuses in any rolling nine-day window. This cap applies across checking products and is based on successful referral-bonus timing.
- The qualifying deposit must be **new money**; a transfer from another Rho-Bank account does not qualify.
- The qualifying deposit must remain in the account for at least 30 days after the qualifying period ends.
- A referral bonus cannot be combined with another new-account promotion or sign-up bonus, and only one referral code may be applied per new account.
- Both accounts must remain in good standing for bonus payment. If the referred account closes within 90 days of opening, the referral bonus may be reversed.
- Referrer tenure is calculated from the opening date of the earliest Rho-Bank checking account. The account type currently held does not change that calculation and does not restrict referring a different checking product.
- `COMPLETE` means the referred party opened the account and met the criteria for the referral bonus.

Annual caps in this catalog are product-specific. Do not consume World Blue's annual cap with completed referrals for Hunter Green, Navy Blue, Cobalt Blue, or another product.
