# Covered savings APY rules

Percentages below are annual percentage points. Add selected applicable components to the base/tier APY before converting the resulting APY to a daily periodic rate. Rates and qualifications must be confirmed for the relevant statement period.

## Silver Account

- Base tier: 2.5% when daily ending balance is below $10,000.
- Higher tier: 4.0% when daily ending balance is at least $10,000.
- Interest is compounded daily and credited monthly.
- Card bonuses: Bronze Rewards 0%; Silver Rewards 0.1%; Gold Rewards 0.5%; EcoCard 2.2%; Green Rewards 0%; Crypto-Cash Back 0.5%. Separate Silver benefits documentation identifies Diamond Elite Card at 0.25% and Platinum Rewards Card at 0.2%; neither can exceed the EcoCard amount in the listed table.
- Covered checking boost: Bluest + Silver = 0.45%.
- A 0.025% relationship bonus may apply when relationship criteria are met.

## Silver Plus Account

- Tier 1: 3.0% below $15,000.
- Tier 2: 4.5% at or above $15,000.
- Direct deposit adds 0.25% for a period in which it is active.
- Card bonuses: Bronze Rewards 0.15%; Silver Rewards 0.15%; Gold Rewards 0.2%; Platinum Rewards 0.15%; Diamond Elite Card 0.4%; EcoCard 0.45%; Green Rewards 0.1%; Crypto-Cash Back 0%.
- Covered checking boost: Blue + Silver Plus = 0.35%.
- A 0.025% relationship bonus may apply when relationship criteria are met.
- Interest is compounded daily and credited monthly.

## Platinum Account

- Base APY: 6.5%.
- Card bonuses: Bronze Rewards 0%; Silver Rewards 0%; Gold Rewards 0.15%; Platinum Rewards 0.25%; Diamond Elite Card 0.35%; EcoCard 0%; Green Rewards 0%; Crypto-Cash Back 0%.
- Covered checking boosts: Blue + Platinum = 0.8%; Light Green + Platinum = 0.65%.
- Interest is compounded daily and credited monthly.

## Diamond Elite Account

- Base APY: 7.5%.
- Card bonuses: Bronze Rewards 0%; Silver Rewards 0%; Gold Rewards 0%; Platinum Rewards 0.1%; Diamond Elite Card 0.5%; EcoCard 0%; Green Rewards 0%; Crypto-Cash Back 0.15%.
- Covered checking boosts: Light Green + Diamond Elite = 0.2%; Evergreen + Diamond Elite = 0.15%.
- Interest is compounded daily and credited monthly.

## Selection and linkage policy

Only the highest applicable active card bonus is applied. Only the highest applicable boost from active, linked, qualifying checking accounts is applied. These two selected components can stack with the base/tier APY and a separately applicable relationship bonus. Listed checking boosts are valid only for the named checking-to-savings pairing and when both accounts are linked under the same customer profile, open, and in good standing.

A rate component not documented above must not be assumed. In particular, do not infer that an account link exists merely because both products are held by the customer.
