# Covered savings APY rules

Percentages are annual percentage points. Add only documented and verified components to the base/tier APY before deriving a daily periodic rate. Confirm that the rates and qualifications applied during the statement period under review.

## Silver Account

- 2.5% for a daily ending balance below $10,000; 4.0% at or above $10,000.
- Interest compounds daily and is credited monthly.
- Card bonuses: Bronze Rewards 0%; Silver Rewards 0.1%; Gold Rewards 0.5%; Platinum Rewards 0.2%; Diamond Elite Card 0.25%; EcoCard 2.2%; Green Rewards 0%; Crypto-Cash Back 0.5%.
- Documented checking pairing: Bluest checking + Silver savings, 0.45%.
- A 0.025% relationship bonus may apply when its criteria are met.

## Silver Plus Account

- 3.0% below $15,000; 4.5% at or above $15,000.
- Verified active direct deposit adds 0.25% for an active period.
- Interest compounds daily and is credited monthly.
- Card bonuses: Bronze Rewards 0.15%; Silver Rewards 0.15%; Gold Rewards 0.2%; Platinum Rewards 0.15%; Diamond Elite Card 0.4%; EcoCard 0.45%; Green Rewards 0.1%; Crypto-Cash Back 0%.
- Documented checking pairing: Blue checking + Silver Plus savings, 0.35%.
- A 0.025% relationship bonus may apply when its criteria are met.

## Platinum Account

- Base APY: 6.5%; interest compounds daily and is credited monthly.
- Card bonuses: Bronze Rewards 0%; Silver Rewards 0%; Gold Rewards 0.15%; Platinum Rewards 0.25%; Diamond Elite Card 0.35%; EcoCard 0%; Green Rewards 0%; Crypto-Cash Back 0%.
- Documented checking pairings: Blue checking + Platinum savings, 0.8%; Light Green checking + Platinum savings, 0.65%.

## Diamond Elite Account

- Base APY: 7.5%; interest compounds daily and is credited monthly.
- Card bonuses: Bronze Rewards 0%; Silver Rewards 0%; Gold Rewards 0%; Platinum Rewards 0.1%; Diamond Elite Card 0.5%; EcoCard 0%; Green Rewards 0%; Crypto-Cash Back 0.15%.
- Documented checking pairings: Light Green checking + Diamond Elite savings, 0.2%; Evergreen checking + Diamond Elite savings, 0.15%.

## Selection, linkage, and eligibility

Use only the highest applicable active card bonus and only the highest applicable qualifying checking boost. Bonuses do not stack within either category. A selected card bonus and checking boost can stack with the base/tier rate and a separately verified relationship bonus.

A checking percentage applies only to its exact named pairing where the accounts are linked under the same customer profile, open, in good standing, and otherwise eligible. Ownership of both accounts in an account-list result is not evidence of a link.

Light Green is available only while its primary holder remains age 13 through 24. Do not include its Platinum or Diamond Elite percentage in an expected APY when verified age evidence creates an unresolved eligibility conflict.
