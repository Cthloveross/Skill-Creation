Use only after confirming that the product is available to the customer and that all current eligibility requirements are met. Credit-card bonus values below are account-specific and are additive to the stated savings APY, but bonuses from multiple cards do not stack: only the highest applicable card bonus may be used.

## Known card facts useful for eligibility discussion

- **EcoCard:** no minimum credit score requirement is documented; annual fee is $50.00. It requires standard identity and income information through its application process. Approval is not guaranteed merely because no minimum score is documented.
- **Bronze Rewards Card:** documented minimum credit score is 640 and annual fee is $0.00.
- **Silver Rewards Card:** documented minimum credit score is 680 and annual fee is $0.00.
- **Gold Rewards Card:** documented minimum credit score is 720, requires an active Rho-Bank+ premium subscription, and has a $0.00 annual fee.

If a customer does not know their score, do not mark score-threshold cards as eligible without another supported eligibility determination. A product with no documented score minimum can be discussed as an application option but still must be described as subject to approval.

## Linked checking and relationship notes

Only explicitly listed checking/savings pairs qualify for a linked-checking APY boost. Light Blue checking is not among the listed qualifying checking pairings, so no linked-checking APY boost is established for it. Some account documentation describes a 0.025% relationship bonus, but apply it only when the relevant relationship criteria are verified. It may stack with a qualifying checking boost and the single highest credit-card bonus.

## Personal savings opening constraints

Before opening a personal savings account, the customer must be verified, have an active Rho-Bank checking account held at least 14 days, hold fewer than five personal savings accounts, and have no accounts in collections or with negative balances. The exact official account class submitted to the opening tool must end in `Account` (for example, `Silver Account` or `Gold Plus Account`).
