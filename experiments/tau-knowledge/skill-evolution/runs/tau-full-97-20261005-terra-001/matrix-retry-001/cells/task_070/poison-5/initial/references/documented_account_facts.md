# Documented account facts for recommendation use

This reference summarizes only the supplied knowledge. It is not a substitute for checking the current conversation and supplied time.

## Lime Green

- Overdraft fee: **$0.00**.
- Monthly maintenance fee: **$25.00**.
- Fee waiver: balance at or above **$15,000**, as defined for the account; the fee may apply if the balance is below that amount during the evaluation period.
- Minimum balance requirement: end-of-day balance of at least **$5,000** to maintain benefits and avoid balance-related charges that can trigger below the requirement.
- Eligible out-of-network ATM-fee rebates: up to **$25 per month**. Eligible fees are rebated until the cap is reached; fees above the cap remain the customer's responsibility. Fees and credits post as separate activity line items.
- Domestic non-Rho ATM fee: **$1.00 per withdrawal**.
- Foreign ATM withdrawal fee: **1.5%** of the withdrawal amount, subject to a **$2.50** minimum.
- The source says ATM operator charges are not established as account fees or eligible rebates; do not promise they are reimbursed.
- Interest: **1.5% APY**, compounded daily.
- Dedicated-manager support: secure messages receive a response within two business hours; priority-line maximum expected wait is six minutes.

## Sky Blue

- Product eligibility: company must be within **four years of formation**; stated funding requirement is 0.
- Free period: **six months**; monthly maintenance fee after that period: **$25.00**.
- Overdraft fee: **$0.00**.
- Out-of-network ATM-fee rebates: up to **$15 per month**.
- Domestic out-of-network ATM fee: **$1.50** per transaction.
- International ATM fee: **2%** of withdrawal amount, **$3.00** minimum.
- Mobile check deposit daily limit: **$25,000**; APY: **1.25%**, compounded daily.

If the formation date is unavailable, Sky Blue's product-specific company-age condition is unconfirmed. It should not be presented as a confirmed fit solely because it is promotion-ranked.

## November 2025 promotion

The internal promotion is active from **2025-11-01 through 2025-11-30**. When multiple accounts meet **all** stated customer requirements, the documented order is:

1. Sky Blue
2. Lime Green
3. Other qualifying accounts only if neither Sky Blue nor Lime Green meets the requirements.

The promotion explicitly does not permit recommending a promoted account that fails a customer requirement.

## General business-checking opening requirements

Before opening a business checking account, the supplied procedure requires all of the following:

1. Customer identity is verified.
2. Customer has at least one existing personal checking account with status OPEN.
3. Customer does not exceed six business checking accounts.
4. Customer has no accounts with status CLOSED.
5. Customer's existing checking account balance is at least $500.

The documented opening procedure then confirms the desired `account_class` and uses the documented `open_bank_account_4821` workflow. Do not assert that a product is opened or that these requirements are satisfied without actual verification.
