# Direct-bonus response checklist

Use this checklist after reading the current task evidence and before sending an informational sign-up-bonus comparison.

## Date and applicability

- Identify the successful observed current date.
- Compare that date inclusively against each complete documented offer window.
- Apply stated audience, new-customer, invitation, and account-status facts.
- Treat missing customer facts as conditional; do not fabricate eligibility.

## Direct-bonus content

For every direct points, cash-back, or statement-credit bonus that is presented, state:

- product name;
- direct-bonus amount and unit;
- offer/application or account-opening window;
- qualifying purchase threshold and period;
- all documented eligibility and good-standing conditions;
- annual fee when the user requested fees; and
- exclusions and fulfillment timing if they materially affect qualification and are documented.

## Points conversion

When a conversion is documented, write it as explicit arithmetic:

`[points] × $[documented dollars per point] per point = about $[result]`

Then name the documented redemption channel. Do not call the original point amount a dollar amount.

## Decision support

If the customer has not stated whether they can meet the required spend, do both of the following:

1. Present the active offer and its qualification terms anyway.
2. Make the spend threshold and deadline the key decision point, and invite the customer to estimate eligible purchases before applying.

## Avoid

- Do not say the evidence is unavailable if supplied documents contain the terms.
- Do not repeat already answered intake questions.
- Do not characterize an expired bonus as current.
- Do not recommend an APR promotion, fee waiver, or ongoing earning rate as a points/cash sign-up bonus.
- Do not perform an application or other account action.
