# Direct-bonus response checklist

Use this checklist after reading the current task's supplied evidence and before sending an informational sign-up-bonus comparison.

## Must answer, not defer

- Read the supplied offer and product documents; they are evidence even without a catalog-search tool.
- If the conversation already provides the date and eligibility facts, send the actual result now.
- Do not say that terms or documents are unavailable when supplied evidence contains them.
- Do not ask again for customer facts already answered.

## Date and applicability

- Identify the successful observed current date.
- Compare that date inclusively with each complete documented offer window.
- Apply stated audience, new-customer, invitation, and account-status facts.
- Treat missing customer facts as conditional; do not fabricate eligibility.

## Direct-bonus content

For every direct points, cash-back, or statement-credit bonus presented, state:

- product name and current status;
- direct-bonus amount and unit;
- offer/application or account-opening window;
- qualifying purchase threshold and period;
- documented new-customer, invitation, approval, open-account, and good-standing conditions;
- annual fee when the user asks about fees or prefers a lower fee; and
- material exclusions and fulfillment timing when documented.

## Points conversion

When a conversion is documented, write explicit arithmetic:

`[points] × $[documented dollars per point] per point = about $[result]`

Name the documented redemption channel. Do not call the original point amount a dollar amount.

## Decision support

If the customer has not stated whether they can meet required spend:

1. Present the active applicable offer and all qualification terms anyway.
2. State that the required spend and deadline are the key decision point.
3. Invite the customer to estimate eligible purchases before applying.

## Avoid

- Do not characterize an expired bonus as current.
- Do not recommend an APR promotion, fee waiver, or ongoing earning rate as a points/cash sign-up bonus.
- Do not present invitation-only terms as applicable without the required invitation.
- Do not promise approval, a credit limit, an offer extension, or qualification beyond the evidence.
- Do not perform an application or other account action.
