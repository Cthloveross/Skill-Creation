# Documented Travel-Checking Facts

Use these facts for comparison only when their conditions match the customer's planned usage. Product availability and account-opening eligibility must still be checked before opening.

## Green Fee-Free Account

- Monthly maintenance fee: $22.50.
- The maintenance fee is waived with a $150 minimum daily balance during the statement cycle.
- Rho-Bank foreign ATM withdrawal fee: $0.00.
- Rho-Bank out-of-network ATM withdrawal fee: $0.00.
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit: $500.
- A third-party ATM operator can separately charge a fee.

For a customer who maintains the $150 minimum daily balance and whose withdrawals do not exceed the daily limit, the documented Rho-Bank maintenance, foreign-ATM, and out-of-network ATM fees are $0.00. This does not establish third-party operator charges.

## Purple Account

- Monthly maintenance fee: $15.00, waived with a $3,750 minimum daily balance.
- Rho-Bank foreign ATM withdrawal fee: $0.00.
- Global ATM operator-fee rebates: up to $30 per month for qualifying posted fees.
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit: $1,000.
- Separately documented Rho-Bank out-of-network ATM withdrawal fee: $2.50 per withdrawal.

Do not assume an operator-fee rebate is a Rho-Bank fee waiver. Verify qualification, posting, and the monthly cap before subtracting a rebate from a comparison. Determine whether the documented out-of-network fee applies to the specific planned foreign withdrawal before adding it.

## Other documented options with relevant limits or fees

- **Blue Account:** foreign ATM fee is the greater of 3% of the USD-equivalent withdrawal or $5.00; daily ATM limit is $500.
- **Green Account (checking):** foreign ATM fee is the greater of 3% of the USD-equivalent withdrawal or $5.00; daily ATM limit is $600. Its $22.50 monthly maintenance fee requires a $1,350 minimum daily balance for waiver.
- **Light Blue Account:** two free foreign ATM withdrawals per month; each later foreign ATM withdrawal that month costs $4.00. Its daily ATM limit is $400.
- **Gold Years Account:** restricted to customers at least 62. Foreign ATM fee is $3.50 per transaction, waived with an account balance of at least $10,000 at withdrawal; daily ATM limit is $600.
- **Dark Green Account:** primary account holder must be at least 17 and can maintain the account through age 26. Foreign ATM fee is 2.5% of the amount, capped at $6.00 per transaction; daily ATM limit is $300.
- **Bluest Account:** requires at least $75,000 to open. A $75 monthly maintenance fee applies below the $112,500 daily balance needed to keep benefits active. Documented foreign ATM fee is $0.00.

No conclusion about a product's complete cost should be made from a missing fee field. Third-party ATM operator surcharges remain separate unless a product's documented rebate explicitly and validly offsets them.
