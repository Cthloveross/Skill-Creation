# Business Checking Referral Terms Reference

Use these terms for product comparison. A referrer bonus is paid to the existing customer; it is not the welcome bonus paid to the new business.

## General requirements and restrictions

- Referrer tenure is measured from the opening date of the earliest Rho-Bank checking account, regardless of the product currently held.
- A referrer can receive no more than two referral bonuses in any rolling nine-day window across all checking-account types. Evaluate the limit with exact successful-bonus timestamps. A third or later referral in that window is automatically denied and cannot be reinstated in that window.
- The referred business must be a new Rho-Bank customer with no existing checking or savings account and no account closed within the prior 12 months.
- The referrer and referred business cannot share a registered address. A referred business must have a different primary owner from every existing Rho-Bank business account, based on the primary authorized signer's SSN.
- A qualifying deposit must be new money, not a transfer from a Rho-Bank account, and must remain for at least 30 days after the qualifying period ends.
- A referral cannot stack with another new-account promotion or sign-up bonus, and only one referral code may be applied to a new account.
- Both accounts must remain in good standing for a bonus to be paid. If the referred account closes within 90 days of opening, the bonus may be clawed back.
- A referrer may direct a business to a different product from the referrer's current account if the relevant tenure threshold is met.

## Time-limited general account recommendation priority

From November 1, 2025 through November 30, 2025, general business-checking account recommendations prioritize Sky Blue and then Lime Green only if each satisfies all stated customer requirements. This priority does not override an explicit request for the highest referrer bonus.

## Separate account-opening workflow

A referral is not account opening. If the referred business elects to apply, it must directly complete the account-opening process. Documented business-checking opening eligibility requires verified customer status, at least one OPEN personal checking account, no more than six business checking accounts, no accounts with status CLOSED, and a balance of at least $500 in the existing checking account. Confirm the desired account class before using the authorized opening procedure.
