# Documented product facts for static-balance comparison

Use these facts only for the documented account/card combinations and conditions below. Product approval, customer eligibility, account linkage, and good standing must be verified separately.

## Leading $20,000 candidates

| Combination | Savings facts | Card APY bonus | Annual card fee | Static $20,000 result |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Silver Account + EcoCard | Silver APY is 4.0% at the documented higher tier; the higher-tier threshold is $10,000. Opening minimum $500; ongoing minimum $1,000. | +2.2% | $50 | 6.2%; $1,240 interest; $1,190 net |
| Gold Account + EcoCard | Gold base APY is 5.5%. Gold minimum balance is $10,000. | +0.6% | $50 | 6.1%; $1,220 interest; $1,170 net |
| Silver Plus Account + EcoCard | Tier 2 APY is 4.5% at balances of at least $15,000. Opening minimum $1,000; ongoing minimum $2,500. | +0.45% | $50 | 4.95%; $990 interest; $940 net |
| Green Account (savings) + EcoCard | Green base APY is 4.0%; the EcoCard bonus is 0.5%. Ongoing minimum is $500. | +0.5% | $50 | 4.5%; $900 interest; $850 net |

Silver Account and EcoCard therefore produce the highest net return among these documented, balance-qualified candidates.

## Known balance exclusions for a $20,000 plan

- Gold Plus Account: opening minimum $10,000, but ongoing minimum balance is $25,000. Although its documented base APY is 6.0%, a $20,000 constant balance does not meet the ongoing requirement.
- Platinum Account: opening deposit minimum is $25,000 and ongoing minimum is $50,000.
- Platinum Plus Account: opening deposit minimum is $50,000 and ongoing minimum is $100,000.
- Diamond Elite Account: documented minimum balance is $250,000.

## Policy and card terms

- Where more than one Rho-Bank card might provide a savings APY bonus, only the highest applicable credit-card bonus applies; bonuses do not stack.
- EcoCard has a $50 annual fee. Its application requires standard identity and income information; the documented minimum credit-score requirement is 0, but an application still must be submitted and approved.
- Savings eligibility requires a verified customer with an active Rho-Bank checking account held for at least 14 days, fewer than five personal savings accounts, and no accounts in collections or with negative balances.
- A Light Blue checking account is not among the documented checking/savings pairings that receive a linked-checking APY boost. Do not add a checking boost for it.
