# Evidence-derived checking referral terms

## Universal checking referral terms

- Referrers may receive no more than two referral bonuses in a rolling nine-day window across all checking account types. The window uses the exact timestamps of recent successful bonuses. A third or subsequent referral in that window is automatically denied and cannot be reinstated within that window.
- The referred party must be a new Rho-Bank customer with no existing checking, savings, or recently closed account in the previous 12 months.
- Individual referrers and referred people may not share a registered address.
- Referred people must be age 18 or older except that Light Green permits minors with a guardian.
- A referred business must have a different primary owner from every existing Rho-Bank business account, tested using the SSN of the primary authorized signer.
- Qualifying deposits must be new money, not transfers from another Rho-Bank account, and must remain for at least 30 days after the qualifying period ends.
- Referral offers cannot be combined with another new-account promotion or sign-up bonus. One referral code applies per new account.
- A bonus may be clawed back if the referred account closes in 90 days. Both accounts must be in good standing.
- Referrer tenure always begins on the opening date of the referrer's earliest Rho-Bank checking account, irrespective of current product type.

## Product terms

### Light Green

- Referral rewards: referrer $15; referred person $25.
- Annual maximum: 3 referral bonuses.
- Account age range: 13 through 24 for the primary account holder.
- Qualifying deposit: at least $100 within 90 days after opening.
- Referrer tenure: first checking account opened at least 14 days earlier.

### Gold Years

- Referral rewards: referrer $50; referred person $75.
- Annual maximum: 6 referral bonuses.
- Referred person must be at least 62.
- Qualifying deposit: $1,000 within 90 days after opening.
- Referrer tenure: 30 days.

### Sky Blue

- Referral rewards: referrer $150; referred startup $250.
- Annual maximum: 8 referral bonuses.
- Startup account eligibility includes company formation within 4 years; the stated funding requirement is 0.
- Qualifying deposit: at least $10,000 within 90 days after opening.
- Referrer tenure: 45 days.

## Status meanings

- `COMPLETE`: account opened and bonus criteria met.
- `IN_PROGRESS`: account opened; qualifying deposit requirement remains.
- `NO_PROGRESS`: no application has been started.
- `APPLIED`: application submitted and awaiting a decision.
- `REJECTED`: denied, including for cap or eligibility reasons.
- `ERROR`: a system error occurred.
