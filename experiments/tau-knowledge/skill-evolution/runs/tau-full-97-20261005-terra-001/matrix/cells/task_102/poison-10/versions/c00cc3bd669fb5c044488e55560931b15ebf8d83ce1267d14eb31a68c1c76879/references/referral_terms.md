# Evidence-derived checking referral terms

## Shared terms

- No more than two successful referral bonuses may occur in a rolling nine-day window across all checking account types. The window is timestamp-based; a third or later referral in that window is automatically denied and cannot be reinstated in that window.
- The referred party must be new to Rho-Bank, with no current checking or savings account and no account closed in the prior 12 months.
- The referrer and an individual referred cannot share a registered address.
- An individual referred must generally be at least 18. Light Green permits minors with a guardian.
- A business referral requires a new Rho-Bank business and a primary authorized signer whose SSN is not associated with an existing Rho-Bank business account.
- The qualifying deposit must be new money, not a transfer from a Rho-Bank account, and must remain for at least 30 days after the qualifying period ends.
- Referral offers cannot stack with another new-account promotion or sign-up bonus; only one referral code applies per new account.
- Closure within 90 days may cause a bonus clawback, and both accounts must remain in good standing.
- Referrer tenure begins on the opening date of the earliest Rho-Bank checking account, regardless of current product.

## Product terms

### Light Green
- Referrer/referred rewards: $15 / $25.
- Annual maximum: 3 completed referral bonuses.
- Primary holder age: 13 through 24.
- Deposit: at least $100 within 90 days of opening.
- Referrer tenure: at least 14 days from first checking-account opening.

### Gold Years
- Referrer/referred rewards: $50 / $75.
- Annual maximum: 6 completed referral bonuses.
- Referred individual age: at least 62.
- Deposit: $1,000 within 90 days of opening.
- Referrer tenure: at least 30 days from first checking-account opening.

### Sky Blue
- Referrer/referred rewards: $150 / $250.
- Annual maximum: 8 completed referral bonuses.
- Startup formation: within 4 years; stated funding requirement is 0.
- Deposit: at least $10,000 within 90 days of opening.
- Referrer tenure: at least 45 days from first checking-account opening.
