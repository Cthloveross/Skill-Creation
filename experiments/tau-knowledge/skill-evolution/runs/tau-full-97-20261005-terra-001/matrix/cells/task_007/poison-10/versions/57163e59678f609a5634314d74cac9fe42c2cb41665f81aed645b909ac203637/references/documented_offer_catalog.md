# Supplied Rho-Bank Offer Comparison Facts

Use these facts only with the observed comparison date and the customer's stated facts. This reference summarizes the supplied documentation for a cash-back/points sign-up-bonus comparison; it does not establish approval or individual eligibility.

## EcoCard — personal

- New-customer promotion window: 2025-08-01 through 2025-12-15.
- Bonus: 2,000 sustainability points.
- Qualification: at least $5,000 in eligible purchases during the first month after account opening; new customer; account open and in good standing when the bonus is awarded.
- Returns, chargebacks, and disputed transactions reduce qualifying spend. Balance transfers, cash equivalents, and fees do not count.
- Points typically post after the qualifying period closes and transactions have cleared.
- EcoCard sustainability points redeem at $0.01 per point as a statement credit or Rho-Bank checking-account credit. Therefore, 2,000 points have a documented $20 redemption value.
- The product documentation states no credit-score requirement, but all applications and approvals remain subject to the card's application process.

## Diamond Elite Card — personal

- Membership is invitation-only. An invitation is required even if other profile characteristics are strong.
- Invitation promotion window: 2025-11-01 through 2026-01-31.
- An invited customer who accepts during the campaign and makes $50,000 in eligible purchases within one month may receive a $5,000 statement credit and annual-fee waiver.
- The standard annual fee is $495. If the invitation threshold is not met, the fee is not waived and the $5,000 credit is not issued.

## Gold Rewards Card — personal

- Applying requires an active Rho-Bank+ premium subscription and a minimum credit score of 720.
- The supplied materials do not document a current points/cash-back sign-up bonus.

## Silver Rewards Card — personal

- A $500 statement-credit sign-up offer required opening an account from 2025-01-01 through 2025-06-30; it is expired after that period.
- A separate 0% introductory-APR promotion runs 2025-10-15 through 2025-12-31. It is not a cash-back or points sign-up bonus.

## Bronze Rewards Card — personal

- The supplied materials document a 0% introductory APR during the first year for qualifying accounts opened from 2025-09-01 through 2026-02-28.
- They do not document a current points or cash-back sign-up bonus.

## Platinum Rewards Card — personal

- A first-year annual-fee waiver was limited to accounts opened from 2024-06-01 through 2024-12-31. It is expired and is not a points/cash-back sign-up bonus.

## Crypto-Cash Back Card — personal

- The supplied materials state a $660 minimum credit score but do not document a current cash-back or points sign-up bonus.

## Interpretation rules

- Cash-back rewards and EcoCard sustainability points can be represented in system data as “points”; this does not itself make a raw point count a dollar amount.
- Only the EcoCard is a true sustainability-points product in the supplied material. Its documented conversion is $0.01 per point for statement-credit or checking-credit redemption.
- Do not equate 2,000 EcoCard points with a $2,000 statement credit or $2,000 value.
