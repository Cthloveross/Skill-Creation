# Authorized travel-checking comparison

Use this reference when the current authorized product materials are the same as the packaged materials. Recheck runtime product documents if they are available and use a newer authorized term if it conflicts.

## Bluest Account

- Rho-Bank foreign ATM withdrawal fee: $0.00.
- Monthly ATM operator-fee rebates: up to $50.
- Early direct deposit: up to two days early.
- Opening deposit: at least $75,000.
- Daily balance needed to keep benefits active: $112,500.
- Monthly maintenance fee below that daily-balance requirement: $75.
- Third-party ATM operator fees may still apply.

## Purple Account

- Rho-Bank foreign ATM withdrawal fee: $0.00.
- Monthly global ATM operator-fee rebates: up to $30.
- Early direct deposit: up to two days early.
- Monthly maintenance fee: $15.
- Maintenance-fee waiver: $3,750 minimum daily balance.
- Third-party ATM operator fees may still apply.

## Comparison rule

Both accounts meet a request for $0 Rho foreign-ATM withdrawal fees and up-to-two-day early direct deposit. Bluest has the larger documented monthly ATM-fee rebate cap, so it is the stronger candidate on those narrow ATM criteria. Its required $75,000 opening deposit and $112,500 daily-balance/$75-maintenance-fee conditions must be disclosed before a customer selects it. Purple's lower rebate cap and its $15 monthly fee/waiver requirement must also be disclosed. Do not claim either is cheapest overall without facts about the customer's funding and balance behavior.
