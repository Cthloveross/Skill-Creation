# Published APY and recommendation notes

## Green Account (savings)

- Base APY: 4.0%.
- Minimum opening deposit: $100; ongoing minimum balance: $500; daily compounding; paperless statements required.
- An EcoCard provides +0.5% APY while eligible, in good standing, and linked. The published EcoCard terms have no minimum credit-score requirement, require normal identity/income application information, have a $50 annual fee, and list 19.99% purchase APR on carried balances.
- Credit-card bonuses do not stack: use the highest applicable card bonus.

## Evergreen Account (checking)

- Published qualifying boost for linked Green savings: +0.55% APY.
- The checking account's $6 monthly maintenance fee is waived at a $500 minimum daily balance.

## Selection policy

- If several active checking accounts qualify for a savings product, use only the highest qualifying checking boost.
- If several active credit cards supply a savings APY bonus, use only the highest applicable card bonus.
- The chosen checking boost and the chosen card bonus can stack with the savings base rate when their distinct requirements are satisfied.
- Do not use account descriptions or card benefits as proof of approval, activation, good standing, or linkage. Verify these at runtime.

Other product documents may contain different rates, balance thresholds, and eligibility conditions. Compare them only where the records and documents establish that they are applicable to the customer's stated balance and requested products.
