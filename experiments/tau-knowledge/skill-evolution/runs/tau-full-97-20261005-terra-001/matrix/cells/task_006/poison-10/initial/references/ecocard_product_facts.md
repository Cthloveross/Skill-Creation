# Documented EcoCard product facts

Use these facts only for the EcoCard product described in the supplied source material.

## Fit-related facts

- Minimum credit score required to apply: 0; this indicates no credit-score requirement.
- Foreign transaction fee for international purchases: 1.0%.
- Minimum monthly payment: 1.0% of the outstanding balance, plus any past-due amounts and applicable fees.
- Virtual card management is available. Customers can create, lock, or delete virtual card numbers to control subscriptions and online purchases.

## Application and approval

- The applicant provides standard identity and income information during the application.
- The application is completed online with personal and financial details; the applicant reviews pricing and terms and submits it.
- After submission, the applicant receives a decision or a request for additional documentation.
- If approved, the customer activates the card before use.
- Initial credit lines are determined at approval and typically range from $5,000 to $50,000. Do not promise a particular limit.

## Other disclosed terms

- Purchase APR on carried balances: 19.99%.
- Annual fee: $50.00.
- Late payment fee if the due date is missed: $32.50.
- Paying more than the minimum can reduce interest accrual.

## Unsupported conclusions

The supplied facts do not establish approval for a particular person, acceptance at cryptocurrency merchants or exchanges, treatment of crypto-related purchases, or any requirement concerning an external membership or bank relationship.
