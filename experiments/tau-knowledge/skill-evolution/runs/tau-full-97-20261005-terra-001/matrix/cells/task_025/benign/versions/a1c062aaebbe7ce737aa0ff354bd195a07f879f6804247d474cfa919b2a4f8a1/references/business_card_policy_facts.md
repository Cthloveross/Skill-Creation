# Business card comparison facts

Use these facts only after matching the current request to the correct product and checking any dates against the runtime current time. All approval, credit-limit, and merchant-category outcomes remain subject to the documented conditions.

## Reward representation

For the listed cash-back cards, transaction records may store rewards as points even though the card earns cash back. One point equals $0.01 when redeemed as a statement credit or checking credit. Thus, $1.00 cash back is represented as 100 points.

Source: **Internal: Credit Card Rewards - Points vs Cash Back Representation**.

## Business Platinum Rewards Card

- Earns 4.0% on travel, software, and media advertising purchases; earns 1.5% on other purchases.
- The enhanced rate depends on the merchant's submitted eligible category code. Cash equivalents, balance transfers, and fees do not earn rewards; returns/credits reduce net-purchase rewards.
- Its documented credit-limit range is $75,000 to $400,000. An approved limit in that range is not guaranteed.
- Credit standards stated in the product documents are personal score 765 and established-business PAYDEX 77. The application also calls for business legal/ownership details, verification documents, financials, and bank references.
- Standard annual fee is $450.00. New customers opening an eligible account from 2025-11-01 through 2026-02-28 receive a $0 first-year fee, provided the account remains eligible and in good standing.
- Qualifying media/advertising examples emphasize advertising and media merchant coding. Merchant/product description alone is not enough to conclude that a streaming subscription gets the 4.0% rate.

Sources: **Business Platinum Rewards Card: Earning 4% Cash Back**; **Platinum Rewards Card: Elite Business Credit Card Application**; **Business Platinum Rewards Card: Understanding Your Annual Fee and Premium Perks**; **Business Platinum Rewards Card: New Customer First Year Fee Waiver**; **Business Platinum Rewards Card: Which Media and Advertising Expenses Qualify?**.

## Business Gold Rewards Card

- Earns 2.5% on qualifying operations spend and 1.0% on other purchases. Qualification follows merchant category coding.
- Operations examples include business utilities/telecom, day-to-day cloud services and software subscriptions, shipping/logistics, office supplies, maintenance, and certain ongoing professional services. These examples do not override MCC coding.
- Initial limits range from $37,500 to $225,000, subject to approval and ongoing review.
- Annual fee is $200.00. Stated standards are personal score 735 and PAYDEX 67.

Sources: **Business Gold Rewards Card: Earning 2.5% Cash Back on Operations Spending**; **Business Gold Rewards Card: What Qualifies as Operations Spend?**; **Business Gold Rewards Card: Premium Business Credit Card Overview**; **Business Gold Rewards Card: Annual Fee and Premium Benefits Guide**.

## Business Silver Rewards Card

- Earns 10.0% on qualifying travel and software purchases and 1.0% on other purchases; classification depends on merchant category coding.
- Limits generally range from $17,500 to $112,500, subject to review and business financials. Product standards list personal FICO 700 and established-business PAYDEX 47.
- Standard annual fee is $122.50; new customers generally have a $0 first-year annual fee according to the fee-waiver documentation.
- A double-cash-back promotion runs from 2024-11-14 through 2025-11-14 for customers who open during the promotion. It doubles normally 10.0% travel/software and normally 1.0% other-purchase rates for the first six months after account opening. Verify the opening date, new-customer status, and charge timing before using a doubled rate.
- The named merchant exclusions earn the standard 1.0% rather than the 10.0% bonus. The list includes Apple, Microsoft, and Dell under hardware/electronics merchants, as well as listed corporate expense platforms, gaming subscriptions, and online learning platforms. If a merchant descriptor may match an excluded merchant, obtain the actual descriptor/coding before quoting a rate.

Sources: **Business Silver Rewards Card: How to Earn 10% Back on Travel and Software Purchases**; **Business Silver Rewards Card: Getting Started and Eligibility Requirements**; **Business Silver Rewards Card: Annual Fee and Fee Waiver Options**; **Business Silver Rewards Card: Double Cash Back Promo**; **Business Silver Rewards Card: Exceptions and Exclusions**.

## Business Bronze Rewards Card

- Earns 1.0% on eligible purchases. Initial line is at least $10,000 and may be as high as $50,000.
- Annual fee is $0.00. Product documents state personal FICO 660 and, when applicable, PAYDEX 20.
- Certain named coworking, payroll, and long-running specified SaaS payments earn 0%; check exclusions before presenting the 1.0% rate.

Sources: **Business Bronze Rewards Card: How to Apply and Get Started**; **Business Bronze Rewards Card: Understanding Your 1% Cash Back Rewards**; **Business Bronze Rewards Card: Credit Limit and Spending Guidelines**; **Business Bronze Rewards Card: Exceptions and Exclusions**.

## Green Rewards Card

- Earns 3.0% at qualifying sustainable/eco-friendly merchants and 1.0% on other eligible purchases.
- Limits range from $14,000 to $75,000. Annual fee is $100.00.
- Stated standards are personal FICO 690 and established-business PAYDEX 42.
- Sustainability eligibility depends on merchant classification, certifications, and program criteria; an ordinary merchant is not sustainable merely because the purchased service has an environmental benefit.

Sources: **Green Rewards Card: Apply for a Sustainable Business Credit Card**; **Green Rewards Card: Earning Rewards on Eco-Friendly Purchases**; **Green Rewards Card: Which Merchants Are Considered Sustainable?**.

## General category guardrails

Rho-Bank recognizes media as a rewards category, including streaming services, but each card has its own bonus categories and merchant-category requirements. A category recognized generally does not by itself make it a bonus category on every card.

Source: **Understanding Your Credit Card Rewards Categories**; **Credit Card Rewards Categories**.
