# Documented business-account facts

Use these facts only where they match the customer’s stated requirements. The product list is not proof that every possible product was evaluated; do not infer unlisted features.

## Time-bounded recommendation priority

For 2025-11-01 through 2025-11-30 inclusive, when multiple business checking accounts meet all customer requirements:

1. Sky Blue has first recommendation priority.
2. Lime Green has second recommendation priority.
3. Other qualifying accounts may be recommended only if neither prioritized account qualifies.

For the same dates, when multiple business savings accounts meet all customer requirements:

1. Gold Saver Account has first recommendation priority.
2. Silver Plus Saver has second recommendation priority.
3. Other qualifying accounts may be recommended only if neither prioritized account qualifies.

Promotional priority is never a substitute for meeting all customer requirements.

## Checking facts

### Sky Blue

- Available to companies within four years of formation.
- Funding requirement shown in the supplied setup information: $0.
- Overdraft fee: $0.00.
- Daily mobile check-deposit limit: $25,000.
- APY: 1.25%, compounded daily.
- Out-of-network ATM fees rebated up to 15 per month.
- Startup free period: six months; after that period the monthly maintenance fee is $25.00.

### Lime Green

- Daily mobile check-deposit limit: $15,000.
- Overdraft fee: $0.00.
- Monthly maintenance fee: $25.00, waived at a $15,000 balance threshold.
- Minimum balance requirement stated for account benefits: $5,000.
- Domestic outgoing wire fee: $10.00; international outgoing wire fee: $25.00.

## Savings facts

### Gold Saver Account

- Same-day ACH: available.
- Outgoing domestic wire fee: $15.
- Automated sweeps are evaluated daily and aim to maintain at least $1,000 in checking; excess may be moved to savings.
- Integration monthly maintenance fee: $10.00.
- Sweep minimum transfer amount: $100.
- Automatic sweep transfer fee: $0.00.
- Daily mobile check-deposit limit: $10,000.
- Daily savings-to-external-bank transfer maximum: $25,000.
- Standard external transfers typically complete within one business day.

### Silver Plus Saver

- Same-day ACH: available.
- Interest is compounded daily.
- Daily mobile check-deposit limit: $7,500.
- Maximum total daily outbound transfer cap: $15,000.

### Other supplied savings facts

- Gold Plus Saver: same-day ACH is available; standard external transfers typically complete within one business day; outgoing domestic wire fee is $10; up to 30 withdrawals per monthly cycle.
- Platinum Reserve Account: same-day ACH is available; domestic outgoing wire fee is $0; external transfer processing time is stated as 0 business days.
- Diamond Vault: same-day ACH is available and interest is compounded daily.

## Opening eligibility and funding rules

### Business checking account

Before opening a business checking account, confirm all of these requirements:

1. Customer is verified.
2. Customer has at least one existing personal checking account with status OPEN.
3. Customer does not exceed 6 business checking accounts.
4. Customer has no accounts with status CLOSED.
5. Customer’s existing checking account balance is at least $500.

The customer must select the desired official business checking account class before opening.

### Business savings account

Before opening a business savings account, confirm all of these requirements:

1. Customer identity is verified.
2. Customer already has at least one business checking account with status OPEN.
3. Customer has fewer than 4 existing business savings accounts.
4. Customer has no accounts with negative balances.
5. At least one existing OPEN business checking account has been open for at least 30 days.
6. That same business checking account has a balance of at least $2,500.
7. Customer confirms the exact official savings account class, ending with “Account.”

After savings opening, ask whether the customer wants to transfer the opening deposit immediately. Transfer only with authorization, using the qualifying checking account that meets both the tenure and balance thresholds. If the customer declines or a transfer fails, the customer has 30 days to fund the savings account through an internal transfer or external deposit; otherwise the account will be closed.
