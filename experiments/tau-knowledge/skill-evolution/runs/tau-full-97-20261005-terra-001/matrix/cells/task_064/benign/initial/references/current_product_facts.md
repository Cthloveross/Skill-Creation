# Current documented product facts for comparison

Use these facts only where the customer meets every applicable product and account-opening requirement. Rates below are percentage points of APY, not decimal fractions.

## Gold Plus Account

- Base APY: 6.0% on eligible balances.
- Opening deposit minimum: $10,000.
- Ongoing minimum balance: $25,000; a $15 monthly maintenance fee may apply below that amount.
- Interest compounds daily and is credited monthly.
- Credit-card APY bonuses: Bronze +0.15%, Silver +0.10%, Gold Rewards +0.35%, Platinum Rewards +0.20%, Diamond Elite +0.25%, EcoCard +0.10%, Green Rewards +0.05%, Crypto-Cash Back +0.30%.

## Relevant card terms

- **Gold Rewards Card:** minimum credit score 720; active Rho-Bank+ subscription required; annual fee $0.00. Its Gold Plus card bonus is +0.35%.
- **EcoCard:** no stated credit-score requirement (published value 0); annual fee $50.00. Its Gold Plus card bonus is +0.10%.
- **Crypto-Cash Back Card:** minimum credit score 660; annual fee $75.00. Its Gold Plus card bonus is +0.30%.

## Other documented savings comparisons and limitations

- Gold Account: 5.5% base APY and $10,000 ongoing minimum. Gold Rewards Card gives +0.025% for the Gold Account and lowers that account's minimum to $5,000. The documents describe this as the Gold Rewards relationship/card benefit; do not double-count the same +0.025% as both a table bonus and a separate relationship bonus.
- Platinum Account: 6.5% APY; $25,000 opening deposit and $50,000 ongoing balance needed to maintain benefits. A $30,000 maintained balance therefore does not satisfy the documented ongoing benefits threshold under a strict comparison.
- Platinum Plus Account: 7.0% APY; $50,000 opening deposit and $100,000 ongoing minimum.
- Diamond Elite Account: 7.5% APY; $100,000 opening deposit and $250,000 ongoing minimum.
- Silver Account has a documented 2.5% or 4.0% balance-tier APY, but the tier threshold needed to select a rate is not supplied here. Do not rank it with a guessed tier.
- Bronze Account: 2.0% APY; $0 opening and ongoing minimum. It can be included if desired, but it does not change the documented $30,000 Gold Plus result.

## Linked-checking and stacking rules

- Card APY bonuses do not stack: only the highest applicable card bonus applies.
- Checking APY boosts do not stack: only the highest applicable checking boost applies.
- The highest card bonus and highest qualifying checking boost may each be added to the base APY, along with separately documented bonus types.
- The documented Gold Plus checking pairings are Gold Years Account + Gold Plus Account and Green Fee-Free Account + Gold Plus Account. Light Blue Account is not a listed pairing, so no checking APY boost may be added for Light Blue + Gold Plus.

## Savings opening control requirements

Before opening a personal savings account, the customer must be verified, have an active Rho-Bank checking account held for at least 14 days, hold fewer than five personal savings accounts, and have no accounts in collections or with negative balances. The official account class passed to the opening tool must end in `Account` exactly. If not immediately funded, the new savings account has 30 days to be funded or will close.
