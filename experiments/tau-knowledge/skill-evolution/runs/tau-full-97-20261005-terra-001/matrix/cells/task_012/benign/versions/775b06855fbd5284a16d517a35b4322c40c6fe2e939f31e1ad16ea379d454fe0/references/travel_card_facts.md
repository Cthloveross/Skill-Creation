# Evidence-grounded facts for Platinum Rewards Card travel guidance

## Supported facts

- The Platinum Rewards Card has enhanced fraud protection. Real-time monitoring can flag unusual transactions and prompt verification.
- The Platinum card guidance recommends enabling transaction and international-use alerts. Account-alert setup includes transaction, payment, and unusual-activity alerts.
- Customers should keep contact details current for time-sensitive prompts. Fraud-alert handling has a target response within 17 minutes.
- The Platinum Rewards Card has a 0% foreign transaction fee.
- The supplied international-travel guidance says to enable travel notifications **if risk controls require it**.
- Network exchange rates and merchant-applied conversion charges are separate from the bank's 0% foreign transaction fee. Paying in local currency when offered a choice may avoid unfavorable dynamic currency conversion.
- International restrictions are one possible cause of a card decline and may need to be lifted before travel. Other possible causes include fraud protection, merchant problems, an inactive new/replacement card, expiry, and temporary technical issues.
- A new or replacement card may require activation. For an ongoing decline, documented checks include verifying entered card details, retrying, checking activation and expiration, and trying another terminal or merchant when possible.
- Support is a documented option when a card decline persists.

## Deliberate limitations

The supplied materials do not state:

- whether exact departure and return dates are required to set a travel notification;
- whether a notification can be filed before dates are final;
- any app or web navigation, form fields, or a discoverable tool for setting a notification;
- that a travel notification removes an international restriction, prevents a fraud review, or guarantees approval; or
- that any particular customer currently has a restriction, alert, or card problem.

Do not fill these gaps with assumptions. For account-specific workflow confirmation, direct the customer to an actually available notification or support option without inventing a procedure.
