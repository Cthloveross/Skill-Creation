# Product evidence: Platinum Rewards Card international-travel inquiry

Use these facts only for the Platinum Rewards Card. They do not establish a workflow for other products.

## Supported statements

- Enhanced fraud protection is included. Real-time monitoring flags unusual transactions and can prompt verification. Customers are advised to enable transaction and international-use alerts and keep contact details current. Source: `doc_credit_cards_platinum_rewards_card_003`.
- Platinum Rewards Card guidance recommends channeling travel purchases through the card, states a 0% foreign transaction fee, and again refers to keeping contact details current for travel-related fraud prompts. Source: `doc_credit_cards_platinum_rewards_card_007`.
- Initial setup includes account alerts for transactions, payments, and unusual activity, and lists a 0% foreign transaction fee. Source: `doc_credit_cards_platinum_rewards_card_001`.

## Material limitation

The supplied Platinum Rewards Card documents do not say that a formal travel notice is available. They do not provide a formal-notice submission route in the app or website, do not identify an agent tool or process for filing one, and do not state whether exact departure and return dates are required.

A separate Gold Rewards Card document says travel notifications may be enabled when risk controls require them. This is not a documented Platinum-specific formal-notice procedure and must not be used to infer one for the Platinum Rewards Card.

## Safe wording

Say that alerts and current contact details are recommended preparation and that verification prompts may occur. If the customer insists on a formal notice, say the available Platinum guidance cannot confirm or submit one, and escalate for confirmation. Do not characterize alerts as a substitute that has filed or waived a formal notice.
