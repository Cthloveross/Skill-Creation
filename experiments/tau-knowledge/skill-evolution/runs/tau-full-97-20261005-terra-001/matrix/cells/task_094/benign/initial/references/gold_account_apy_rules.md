# Gold Account APY policy reference

Use these facts only after confirming that the disputed account is a Gold savings account and that the related products are active under the same customer profile.

- Gold Account documentation states a base APY of 5.5% and daily compounding, with interest credited monthly.
- Green checking plus Gold savings is an eligible linked-account pairing. Green checking's stated linked-Gold boost is +0.75% APY.
- Gold Account credit-card bonus candidates include: Bronze Rewards +0.15%, Silver Rewards +0.2%, Gold Rewards +0.025%, Platinum Rewards +0.15%, Diamond Elite +0.3%, EcoCard +0.6%, Green Rewards +0.35%, and Crypto-Cash Back +0%.
- If multiple eligible credit cards are active, select only the highest card bonus. Card bonuses do not stack with each other.
- If multiple qualifying checking accounts exist, select only the highest applicable checking boost. Checking boosts do not stack with each other.
- The selected card bonus and selected checking boost can stack with separately applicable relationship and tier bonuses.
- A Gold Rewards Card holder has a separately documented +0.025% Gold Account relationship bonus while the card remains active and associated with the account. Treat that as a relationship component only after verifying the documented eligibility. Its separate relationship nature does not make all card bonuses cumulative.
- Gold documentation describes standard and card-adjusted balance requirements. Review actual daily balances and the applicable product rule before declaring a tier/rate qualified for the entire interest period.

Do not use the illustrative total-rate statement in product prose as a substitute for adding documented percentage-point components. Record every selected component and the evidence supporting it.
