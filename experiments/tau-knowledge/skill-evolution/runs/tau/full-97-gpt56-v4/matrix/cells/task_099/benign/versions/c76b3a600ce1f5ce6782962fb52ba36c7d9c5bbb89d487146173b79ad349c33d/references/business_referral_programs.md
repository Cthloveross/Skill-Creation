# Supported business checking referral terms

These terms are used only for comparing a referrer's potential bonus. General checking referral restrictions also apply to every program.

| Program | Referrer bonus | Referred-business bonus | Annual cap | Required qualifying deposit | Deposit window | Referrer tenure |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Navy Blue | $100 | $75 | 10 | $5,000 | 90 days | 60 days |
| Lime Green | $200 | $150 | 12 | $15,000 | 90 days | 90 days |
| True Blue | $350 | $250 | 15 | $50,000 | 120 days | 90 days |
| Beige | $500 | $350 | 15 | $100,000 | 120 days | 120 days |

All programs: a maximum of two referral bonuses may be received in any rolling nine-day window across checking account types. A qualifying deposit must be new money, not a transfer from another Rho-Bank account, and must remain in the account at least 30 days after the qualifying period ends. The account may be subject to clawback if closed within 90 days of opening; both accounts must remain in good standing. Referral bonuses cannot be combined with other new-account promotions or sign-up bonuses, and one referral code may be applied per new account.

A referred business must be a new Rho-Bank customer without existing checking or savings accounts and without a closed account in the preceding 12 months. It must have a primary owner/primary authorized signer SSN different from every existing Rho-Bank business account. The referrer and referred person cannot be registered at the same address.
