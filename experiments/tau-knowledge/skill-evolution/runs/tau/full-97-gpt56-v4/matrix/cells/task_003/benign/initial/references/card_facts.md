# Available card facts for recommendation

Use these facts only for product guidance. Published ranges and benefits are subject to approval and stated terms.

## Silver Rewards Card

- Minimum stated credit score: 680.
- Annual fee: $0.00.
- Initial approved credit-limit range: $10,000 to $100,000, subject to approval.
- Foreign transaction fee: 2.75% without a premium subscription; 0% with a premium subscription.
- Purchase protection: eligible items for 90 days after purchase, up to $3,000 per claim.
- Standard reward rate: 1.0% outside top categories.
- Enhanced reward rate: 4.0% on eligible travel and software transactions after posting.
- For travel, typical eligible merchant classifications include direct airlines/airfare, hotels, resorts, motels, car rentals and car-sharing, intercity rail, ferries, long-distance buses, qualifying online travel agencies/tour operators that are merchant of record, and rideshare/taxis coded as travel.
- Travel-like purchases may not qualify when coded differently (such as parking, tolls, local transit, some vacation rentals, onboard retail/food, or transactions through processors that mask travel coding). Rewards depend on the merchant's submitted category.
- Virtual card management is not available.

## Gold Rewards Card

- Rho-Bank+ premium subscription is required.
- Minimum stated credit score: 720.
- Annual fee: $0.00.
- Approved credit-limit range: $17,500 to $112,500, determined by underwriting.
- Foreign transaction fee: 0%.
- Purchase protection: 105 days from purchase, up to $7,500 per covered claim.
- Rewards: 2.5% cash back on all purchases.
- Late payment fee: $32.50; carried-balance APR: 17.99%.
- Virtual card management is available.

## Communication cautions

A stated maximum limit describes possible approved capacity, not an assurance of a $100,000 or higher offer. A zero foreign transaction fee conditioned on a subscription applies only while the relevant subscription condition is met. Network exchange rates, merchant conversion charges, and dynamic currency conversion are separate from a bank foreign transaction fee.
