# Evidence-to-comparison rules

Use this reference with the runtime product evidence; it intentionally contains no product catalogue or customer-specific values.

## Eligibility labels

- **Known eligible:** all stated prerequisites relevant to the comparison are affirmatively met, or the product has no such prerequisite in the evidence.
- **Known ineligible:** a stated prerequisite is affirmatively unmet. Exclude it.
- **Unknown:** a prerequisite exists but the customer has not confirmed it. Exclude it from a recommendation framed as the best supported result. It may be mentioned separately as conditional, never as an available choice.

Approval is distinct from eligibility. A customer can be eligible to apply but not approved.

## APY computation

For one account and one year at a constant balance:

- `effective APY = base APY + established fixed/relationship bonus + established qualifying checking bonus + applicable card bonus`
- `annual interest = deposited balance × effective APY / 100`

Selection policies can prohibit stacking. If the facts say only the highest card bonus applies, choose the largest applicable card bonus rather than adding them. Do the same for multiple checking boosts where required. A checking account creates no boost unless the evidence lists its exact pairing with the selected savings account.

## Reward computation

- Cash back: `annual spend × rate / 100`.
- Points: `annual spend × points per dollar × documented dollars per point`.
- For a monthly range, multiply each endpoint by 12, calculate both results, and show a range.
- Use only an ordinary earn rate if merchant/category qualification is absent. Bonus-category earnings can be described as contingent upside but must not silently increase the comparison.

## Promotions and fees

Annual fees reduce return. Include an annual-fee waiver, fee rebate, signup bonus, or promotional interest term only after confirming every stated condition, including dates and qualification thresholds. A full-payment plan removes the need to project purchase APR, but it does not itself eliminate an annual fee.

## Suggested concise answer structure

1. Name one combination and qualify it as the best supported by the known facts.
2. Show effective APY and one-year interest on the stated deposit.
3. Show ordinary rewards from the stated spending range, fee, and total net range.
4. Name only the material reasons other apparently higher-rate products were not used.
5. State the assumptions: balance remains on deposit, card is paid in full, and unverified category bonuses/promotions are not included.
