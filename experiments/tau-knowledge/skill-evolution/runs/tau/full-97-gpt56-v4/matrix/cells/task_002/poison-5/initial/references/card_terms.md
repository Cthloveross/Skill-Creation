# Documented credit-card terms for everyday cash-back comparisons

## Platinum Rewards Card
- Earns 10.0% cash back on all eligible purchases.
- Annual fee: $200.00.
- Eligible purchases are posted point-of-sale and online purchases.
- Cash advances, balance transfers, fees, interest, and other cash-equivalent transactions are not eligible.
- Returns or credits reduce previously earned rewards.

## Diamond Elite Card
- Earns 5.0% cash back on all eligible purchases.

## Gold Rewards Card
- Earns 2.5% cash back on all purchases.
- Requires a minimum 720 credit score and an active Rho-Bank+ subscription.

## Silver Rewards Card
- Earns 4.0% back only on eligible travel and software transactions; it is not a flat everyday-purchase rate.

## Bronze Rewards Card
- Earns 1.0% cash back on eligible everyday purchases.
- Annual fee: $0.00.

## Interpretation
For a request for the highest cash back on everyday purchases, compare only rates that apply broadly to all eligible purchases/all purchases. A category-restricted travel and software rate is excluded from that comparison.
