# Supplied business-card policy reference

Use only the facts applicable to the requested comparison. Merchant category coding controls category rates where stated.

## Business Bronze Rewards Card

- Earns 1.0% cash back on eligible purchases; rewards are based on net transaction amounts after credits or returns.
- Annual fee is $0.00. Starting credit line is at least $10,000.
- Stated personal FICO minimum is 660. An established business PAYDEX of 20 or higher is helpful; it is not required for new businesses.
- A new-customer offer available from 2025-10-01 through 2026-03-31 provides a $500 statement credit after $10,000 in net new purchases posted within the first two months of account opening. The credit appears on a future statement after qualification.

## Business Silver Rewards Card

- Earns 10.0% on travel and software purchases and 1.0% on other purchases. The merchant must be processed under an eligible category for the higher rate.
- Standard annual fee is $122.50. New customers have a first-year fee of $0 when they open during the documented 2025-11-15 through 2026-01-15 waiver window.
- Stated personal FICO minimum is 700; established businesses may qualify using PAYDEX of at least 47. Published approved limits generally range from $17,500 to $112,500.
- A documented double-cash-back promotion applies to accounts opened by 2025-11-14, with a six-month period beginning on the account-opening date. It doubles the normally applicable rate, subject to exclusions.
- The 10% travel/software rate is excluded for Concur, SAP Concur, Expensify, Navan, Apple, Microsoft, Dell, listed gaming subscriptions, and listed online-learning platforms; those purchases earn 1.0% instead.

## Business Gold Rewards Card

- Earns 2.5% on qualifying operations spending and 1.0% on other purchases. Merchant category coding, rather than the purchase description alone, determines the rate.
- Operations examples include utilities, telecommunications, cloud and software subscriptions, shipping/logistics, office supplies, routine premises maintenance, bookkeeping, and payroll administration. Cash-like, peer-to-peer, debt-repayment, entertainment, personal, and non-business transactions are non-qualifying examples.
- Annual fee is $200.00 and carried-balance APR is 17.99%. Initial credit limits start at $37,500 and may reach $225,000 subject to approval and review.
- Stated eligibility minimums are personal credit score 735 and PAYDEX 67.

## Business Platinum Rewards Card

- Earns 4.0% on travel, software, and media advertising purchases when coded to those categories, and 1.5% on other purchases. Cash equivalents, balance transfers, and fees do not earn cash back.
- Standard annual fee is $450.00. The first-year fee is $0 for qualifying new accounts opened from 2025-11-01 through 2026-02-28.
- Stated eligibility minimums are personal credit score 765 and PAYDEX 77 for established businesses. Published approved limits range from $75,000 to $400,000.

## Silver Zoom Card

- Provides 3.0% on eligible transportation and logistics expenses and 1.0% on other eligible purchases. A recurring 10.0% fuel price reduction applies only at participating stations.
- Stated credit profile thresholds are personal credit at or above 680 and business credit at or above 57. Annual membership is $74.50.

## Interpretation

A work vehicle's business use or the fact that it is purchased from a vehicle dealer does not itself establish that it is travel, software, operations, transportation/logistics, media advertising, or another bonus category. Confirm the seller's MCC before representing a category bonus as applicable.

Legacy systems may represent cash-back rewards as points. For the listed cash-back cards, 1 point equals $0.01 when redeemed as a statement credit or checking-account credit.
