# Savings/Card Product Terms for Yield Comparisons

Use these terms only with the current customer facts and applicable current disclosures. A rate, fee, or qualification stated here does not establish that a particular customer qualifies.

## Cross-product rules

- Credit-card APY bonuses are automatically applied only when the credit card and savings account are under the same customer profile.
- Multiple credit-card APY bonuses do **not** stack. Only the highest applicable card bonus applies.
- A checking-linked savings APY boost applies only to a specifically listed checking/savings pairing; it is additive to base APY and any applicable credit-card bonus. The listed pairings do not include Light Blue checking.
- When more than one qualifying checking boost exists, only the highest checking boost applies.
- Product documents describe APY as accruing with daily compounding and normally being credited monthly. For a stated APY, use the APY itself for an annual estimate rather than compounding it a second time.

## Relevant savings products

### Gold Account

- Published base APY: 5.5%; standard minimum balance: $10,000.
- An active, associated Gold Rewards Card reduces the minimum balance requirement to $5,000 and adds a stated +0.025% relationship APY bonus.
- Gold Account card-bonus table: Bronze Rewards +0.15%, Silver Rewards +0.20%, Gold Rewards +0.025%, Platinum Rewards +0.15%, Diamond Elite +0.30%, EcoCard +0.60%, Green Rewards +0.35%, Crypto-Cash Back +0%.
- The Gold Rewards benefits document contains an illustrative statement calling 5.5% plus 0.025% “6.0%.” This conflicts with the explicit rate components. Use the components for arithmetic and flag the discrepancy before making an exact yield promise.

### Silver Plus Account

- Minimum opening deposit: $1,000; ongoing minimum balance: $2,500; Tier 2 threshold: $15,000.
- Tier 1 APY: 3.0%; Tier 2 APY: 4.5%.
- An active direct deposit adds +0.25% to the applicable base tier.
- Card-bonus table: Bronze Rewards +0.15%, Silver Rewards +0.15%, Gold Rewards +0.20%, Platinum Rewards +0.15%, Diamond Elite +0.40%, EcoCard +0.45%, Green Rewards +0.10%, Crypto-Cash Back +0%.

### Green Account (savings)

- Base APY: 4.0%; minimum opening deposit: $100; ongoing minimum balance: $500; paperless statements required.
- EcoCard adds a stated +0.5% bonus while eligible and linked. EcoCard-holder benefits state 15 free withdrawals and up to $20 monthly ATM-fee rebates.
- One EcoCard-holder benefits document gives “4.0% + 0.5% = 4.75%,” which conflicts with the stated components. Use the components for arithmetic and flag the inconsistency.
- Other listed card bonuses: Bronze Rewards +0%, Silver Rewards +0.45%, Gold Rewards +0%, Platinum Rewards +0.35%, Diamond Elite +0.60%, Green Rewards +0.40%, Crypto-Cash Back +0%.

### Bronze Account

- Base APY: 2.0%; opening and ongoing minimum balance: $0; monthly maintenance fee: $0.
- Six free withdrawals per month. A documented excess-withdrawal fee may apply after six; a paper statement fee may apply for paper delivery.
- Card-bonus table includes Gold Rewards +0.25%, Green Rewards +0.20%, Crypto-Cash Back +0.30%, Bronze Rewards +0.10%, and EcoCard +0%.

### Higher-balance products

- Gold Plus: 6.0% APY; $10,000 opening deposit and $25,000 ongoing minimum.
- Platinum: 6.5% APY; $25,000 opening deposit and $50,000 ongoing minimum.
- Platinum Plus: 7.0% APY; $50,000 opening deposit and $100,000 ongoing minimum.
- Diamond Elite Account: 7.5% APY; $250,000 ongoing minimum.

Do not recommend a higher-balance product merely because its APY is higher if the stated deposit cannot meet its documented balance requirement.

## Relevant card conditions and fees

### Gold Rewards Card

- Annual fee: $0.
- Requires an active Rho-Bank+ premium subscription.
- Minimum credit score to apply: 720. Approval remains subject to underwriting.
- The subscription's cost is not provided in the referenced terms. Include it as an unknown cost in a net-benefit comparison unless the customer confirms they already hold it or supplies its incremental cost.

### EcoCard

- Annual fee: $50.
- No minimum credit-score requirement is stated (the stated value is 0); identity and income information are required to apply.
- Approval remains subject to underwriting.

### Diamond Elite Card

- Invitation-only; a credit score of at least 780 is a stated consideration.
- Annual fee: $495.

## Opening a personal savings account

Before an opening, the customer must be verified, have at least one active Rho-Bank checking account, have held checking for at least 14 days, hold fewer than five personal savings accounts, and have no accounts in collections or with negative balances. The account class must be the full official name ending in `Account`. After opening, an immediate opening-deposit transfer needs separate customer authorization; if funding is deferred, the account has a documented 30-day funding window before closure.
