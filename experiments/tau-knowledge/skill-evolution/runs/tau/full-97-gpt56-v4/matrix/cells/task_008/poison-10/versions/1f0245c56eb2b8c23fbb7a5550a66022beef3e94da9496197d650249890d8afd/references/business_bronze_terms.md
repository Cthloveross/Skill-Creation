# Business Bronze Rewards Card — documented general terms

Use these terms only as general documentation for comparison. They do not validate a separate mailed, targeted, invitation-only, or otherwise unverified offer.

## New-customer promotion

- Promotion window: 2025-10-01 through 2026-03-31.
- Qualification: make net new purchases totaling $10,000 within the first two months after account opening.
- Fulfillment: after qualification, a $500 statement credit is applied on a future statement.
- Only net purchases count. Returns and credits reduce qualifying spend.
- Transactions must post within the two-month period to count.
- Progress may be tracked in the account dashboard.

## International purchases

- Purchases outside the United States or with non-USD merchants are subject to a 2.75% foreign transaction fee on the converted amount.

## Credit and eligibility context

- Starting credit line is at least $10,000 and may be higher based on underwriting.
- General application guidance identifies a minimum personal FICO score of 660. A business PAYDEX score of 20 or higher may be considered when available.
- General card terms state 1.0% cash back on eligible purchases, a $0 annual fee, and a variable APR of 20.49% on carried balances.

## Handling constraint

Do not infer that a claim for a different statement-credit amount, a fee waiver, a different duration, or any other mailing-specific condition is valid merely because general product terms exist. Verify the offer against the identified, verified customer account and its applicable promotion before taking action.
