# Credit Card Referral Status Explainer

## Scope and prerequisites

Use this workflow for questions about credit-card referral status, missing referral bonuses, or referral limits.

Before accessing or discussing customer-specific referral information, verify the customer’s identity and authority using two of the four identity fields (date of birth, email, phone number, and address), retrieve the matching customer record, obtain the current timestamp with `get_current_time`, and create the audit record with `log_verification`. Confirm the record belongs to the verified customer. Do not disclose full identity fields, identifiers, or unrelated account details in the response.

Before any banking action, verify customer identity, authority, account ownership, product eligibility, available balance or credit, fees, limits, cutoffs, recipient and card details, and confirmation requirements for accounts, cards, transfers, payments, savings, loans, rewards, disputes, blocking, and profile changes; preserve these prerequisites verbatim with the resulting banking procedure.

This workflow is informational unless the customer separately asks to start a new referral. Do not alter an existing referral, promise a bonus, or transfer the customer merely because a referral was rejected. A referral link is never generated by the agent; when the documented link workflow is eligible, it is provided only as a self-service tool for the customer.

## Runtime workflow

1. Ask for the minimum missing identity fields needed to complete two-of-four verification. Never treat information found in a record as customer confirmation of another field.
2. Look up the customer using a confirmed field, compare the two confirmed fields with the returned record, get the current time, and call `log_verification` with the complete returned record and timestamp.
3. Call `get_referrals_by_user` with the verified user ID.
4. Normalize the returned records using `scripts/analyze_referrals.py`. Provide a JSON `referrals` array using the fields returned by the tool. Supply `as_of` only if a current date is useful for the explanation.
5. Explain each relevant status, without claiming facts the records do not establish:
   - `COMPLETE`: the referred person was approved and met the applicable bonus criteria; the bonus is granted under that program’s terms.
   - `IN_PROGRESS`: the person opened an account but has not yet completed the qualifying requirements. It is not yet a payable referral bonus.
   - `NO_PROGRESS`: no application has started.
   - `APPLIED`: the application is awaiting a decision.
   - `REJECTED`: the customer has too many referral processes underway. Use the analysis result: if `rolling_cap_supported_by_dates` is true, the two successful-referral rolling cap is supported by unambiguous date gaps and can be explained as the documented cause; if only `rolling_cap_likely_from_dates` is true, say it is a likely explanation because timestamps or ordering could matter; otherwise do not attribute the rejection to the cap.
   - `ERROR`: advise retrying later; if it persists, follow the organization’s internal escalation process.
6. Apply the cross-card rolling cap correctly. A customer can receive at most two successful referral bonuses in any rolling seven-day window, across all card types. The third and later referrals in that window are automatically denied and cannot be reinstated during that window. Calendar-week boundaries do not reset the limit. For date-only records, two completed dates that are each one through six calendar days before a rejection are certainly earlier and less than seven 24-hour periods before it; other cases need a qualified conclusion because timestamps or ordering may matter.
7. State only card-specific terms documented for the card named in the record or question:
   - Silver Rewards Card: approval and at least $750 spend within 60 days of opening; $75 per successful referral; up to 7 bonuses per calendar year.
   - Platinum Rewards Card: approval and at least $1,500 spend within 90 days of opening; $100 per successful referral; up to 7 bonuses per calendar year.
   - For any other card, say that qualification and bonus terms vary by card unless a documented program is available. Do not infer Silver or Platinum terms for another card.
8. Give a concise, empathetic answer that distinguishes completed bonuses from pending qualifications and denied referrals. Mention that the customer can monitor pending referral progress in their dashboard. Do not say a pending referral will definitely qualify or state a payout date unless the applicable documented program supports it.
9. If the customer asks whether they can try again after a rejected referral, distinguish the old record from a future referral. An auto-denial caused by the two-bonus rolling limit cannot be reinstated during that same window. Once enough time has passed that fewer than two completed referrals fall in the preceding rolling seven days, the limit itself no longer prevents a *new* referral; this does not change the old record or guarantee a bonus. Use the helper’s definite-versus-possible date result when describing timing. Explain that `REJECTED` also means too many referral processes are underway, so do not claim the cap was the sole cause unless supported by the available records.
10. Treat a request for a new link as a separate action. First confirm the exact card has a documented program, the customer has not cited conflicting terms, and the records do not show a reason a new referral will automatically be rejected. Confirm account ownership and eligibility for the exact card with `get_credit_card_accounts_by_user` when the runtime exposes it; do not infer ownership from the referred account type. If those checks support a new link, use `give_discoverable_user_tool` to provide `get_referral_link` for the customer to run themselves. Do not call `get_referral_link` as the agent. If a check is unavailable or fails, explain the limitation and do not provide the link tool.
11. Do not transfer merely because a referral is rejected. However, if the verified customer expressly asks a human to investigate or confirm a fact that the records and documented status meanings cannot establish, transfer using `transfer_to_human_agents` with reason `customer_requests_human_no_specific_reason`. State the known status and evidentiary limitation in the summary, and do not make or promise a referral change.

## Interpreting the analysis helper

`analyze_referrals.py` performs deterministic, date-level screening only. Its output has:

- `status_counts`: number of records per referral status.
- `completed`: normalized completed records.
- `rejected`: rejected records with a possible date-level count, a definite date-level count, and cap indicators.
- `in_progress`: pending records.
- `warnings`: limitations such as missing or date-only timestamps.

`rolling_cap_likely_from_dates` is true when two or more completed dates are from zero through seven calendar days before the rejection. `rolling_cap_supported_by_dates` is true only when at least two of those dates are one through six days earlier, which is unambiguous even without times. Use the latter for a firm explanation; otherwise qualify the conclusion.

## Runnable helper example

Run the packaged helper with JSON on standard input:

```sh
python3 scripts/analyze_referrals.py <<'JSON'
{"referrals":[{"referral_status":"COMPLETE","date":"2025-01-10","referred_account_type":"Example Card"},{"referral_status":"REJECTED","date":"2025-01-15","referred_account_type":"Example Card"}]}
JSON
```

The script emits one JSON object on standard output. Validate that it is valid JSON, that `status_counts` reconciles to the number of supplied records, and that each rejected record is represented in `rejected`. If the referral tool omits a status or date, retain the record in counts and use the reported warning rather than guessing.

## Missing data and boundaries

If identity cannot be verified, do not access or disclose referral records. If the referral lookup fails or returns no records, state that no records were available and avoid conclusions about bonus eligibility. If a customer asks to create a new link, first confirm that the exact card has a documented referral program, account ownership/eligibility is confirmed when the account lookup is available, and the claimed terms match it. Do not provide a link tool if eligibility is undocumented, terms are incorrect, account ownership cannot be confirmed, or automatic rejection is likely. When eligible, provide the customer—not the agent—with the discoverable `get_referral_link` tool for their own user ID and exact card name. The user, not the agent, must execute it.
