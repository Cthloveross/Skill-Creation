# Rho-Bank personal-card facts for cash-back recommendations

Use only these facts for this Skill.

## Gold Rewards Card
- Card annual fee: $0.00.
- Flat cash back: 2.5% on all purchases.
- Requires an active Rho-Bank+ premium subscription and a minimum credit score of 720.
- Rho-Bank+ is listed separately at $4.99/month; whether that costs the customer anything depends on their arrangement.
- Source: `doc_credit_cards_gold_rewards_card_001`, `doc_personal_subscriptions_rho_bank_plus_001`.

## Bronze Rewards Card
- Card annual fee: $0.00.
- Minimum credit score: 640.
- Flat cash back: 1.0% on eligible purchases.
- Standard APR is 20.49%; first-year introductory APR for new customers is 0%.
- Source: `doc_credit_cards_bronze_rewards_card_001`, `doc_credit_cards_bronze_rewards_card_002`.

## Silver Rewards Card
- Card annual fee: $0.00.
- Minimum credit score: 680.
- General purchases outside top categories earn 1.0% back.
- Eligible travel and software/SaaS transactions can earn 4.0% when merchant classification qualifies.
- Source: `doc_credit_cards_silver_rewards_card_001`, `doc_credit_cards_silver_rewards_card_002`, `doc_credit_cards_silver_rewards_card_003`.

## Platinum Rewards Card
- Earns 10.0% cash back on eligible purchases according to its rewards document, but that document says to consider its $200 annual fee.
- Its first-year $0 annual-fee promotion was only for applications submitted from 2024-06-01 through 2024-12-31. It cannot be retroactively applied outside that window; standard $200 annual fee applies after the first anniversary.
- Source: `doc_credit_cards_platinum_rewards_card_002`, `doc_credit_cards_platinum_rewards_card_009`.

## Diamond Elite Card
- Invitation-only; a score of at least 780 is suggested, but invitation is not guaranteed.
- Standard annual fee: $495.00 unless a qualifying invitation promotion applies.
- A rewards document describes 5.0% cash back on eligible purchases; another invitation promotion requires $50,000 spend for a fee waiver and $5,000 statement credit. Do not assume the customer has that invitation or promotion.
- Source: `doc_credit_cards_diamond_elite_card_001`, `doc_credit_cards_diamond_elite_card_003`, `doc_credit_cards_diamond_elite_card_006`.

## Important interpretation
Transaction-system reward balances may be stored as points for cash-back cards. For statement-credit or Rho-Bank checking redemption, 1 point equals $0.01. This does not change the advertised percentage cash-back rates above. Source: `doc_credit_cards_credit_cards_(general)_006`.
