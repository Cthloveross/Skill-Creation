# Product terms for comparison

This reference contains only documented terms relevant to choosing among the listed products. Protection remains subject to the applicable terms, conditions, and exclusions. Credit limits are subject to underwriting.

## Platinum Rewards Card (personal)

- Eligible purchases earn **10.0% cash back in all categories**; eligible point-of-sale and online purchases qualify. Cash advances, balance transfers, fees, interest, and cash-equivalent transactions do not.
- Foreign transaction fee: **0%**.
- Typical approved credit limits: **$25,000 to $150,000**.
- Purchase protection: eligible items, up to **135 days** after purchase and **$17,500 per claim**.
- Annual fee: **$200.00**.
- Stated minimum credit score for applicants: **750**.

## Gold Rewards Card (personal)

- Cash back on all purchases: **2.5%**.
- Foreign transaction fee: **0%**.
- Purchase protection: **105 days** from purchase, up to **$7,500 per covered claim**.
- Approved limits typically range from **$17,500 to $112,500**.
- Requires an active Rho-Bank+ premium subscription. Minimum stated credit score: **720**.
- Annual fee: **$0.00**.

## Silver Rewards Card (personal)

- Purchases outside top categories earn **1.0%** back.
- Foreign transaction fee is **2.75% without a premium subscription** and **0% with a premium subscription**.
- Purchase protection: **90 days**, up to **$3,000 per claim**.
- Initial credit limits typically range from **$10,000 to $100,000**.
- Annual fee: **$0.00**. Minimum stated credit score: **680**.

## Business Platinum Rewards Card (business)

- Travel, software, and media-advertising purchases earn **4.0%**. Other purchases earn **1.5%**. Category treatment depends on merchant coding.
- Foreign transaction fee: **0%**.
- Purchase protection: up to **$17,500 per eligible claim**. The supplied terms do not state a protection-window duration.
- Approved credit limits: **$75,000 to $400,000**.
- It is a business card. Stated application criteria include a minimum personal credit score of **765**, minimum established-business PAYDEX of **77**, business financials/bank references, and underwriting.
