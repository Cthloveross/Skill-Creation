# Verified checking-product facts for comparison

Use these facts only for the named products and only where relevant to the customer's request.

## Blue Account

- Account overdraft fee: $0.00.
- Optional overdraft protection can transfer funds from an eligible linked account to cover a shortfall.
- Each protection-triggered transfer costs $12.50.
- Early direct deposit is available up to 1 day before payday.
- The optional protection enrollment requires choosing a linked funding source and accepting the per-transfer-fee disclosure.

## Light Blue Account

- Account overdraft fee: $0.00.
- Listed overdraft-protection transfer fee: $0.00.
- Early direct deposit: 0 days before payday.
- Monthly maintenance fee: $0.00.

## Important comparison note

A $0 account overdraft fee does not establish whether a product can automatically transfer funds from another account. Verify both the coverage mechanism and its fee. Similarly, an account that offers 0 days of early direct deposit does not meet a request for funds at least one day before payday.

## Personal checking opening prerequisites

A customer must be verified, be at least 18 years old, have no more than four personal checking accounts, and have no checking account closed for cause in the last six months. The official account class must be supplied in full and end in `Account` before using the documented opening capability.
