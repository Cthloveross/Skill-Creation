## Comparison notes

- Gold's 2.5% is the highest documented broad cash-back rate in this reference among cards with a $0 card annual fee. Its subscription and minimum-score requirements still apply.
- Silver's 4.0% rate is not a general-spend rate: it applies only when an eligible transaction is categorized as travel or software/SaaS. Transactions outside its top categories earn 1.0%.
- Eligibility requirements are documented product conditions, not a decision about whether any particular person will be approved.
- A $0 card annual fee does not establish the cost of a required subscription; no subscription price is stated here.
