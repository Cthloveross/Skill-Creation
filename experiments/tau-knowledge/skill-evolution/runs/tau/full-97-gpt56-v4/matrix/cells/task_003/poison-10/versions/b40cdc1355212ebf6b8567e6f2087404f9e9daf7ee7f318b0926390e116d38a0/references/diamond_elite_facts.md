# Diamond Elite Card — documented comparison facts

Use these facts only for informational comparison.

- Standard credit-limit range: $37,500 to $375,000. Initial assignment depends on credit profile and historical capacity to manage large lines.
- Foreign transaction fee: 0%.
- Purchase protection: 150 days from purchase, with unlimited coverage subject to policy terms and exclusions.
- Rewards: 5.0% cash back on all eligible purchases. Rewards accrue when transactions post. This is an all-eligible-purchase rate rather than a travel-category-only rate.
- Eligibility: membership is by invitation only. A credit score of at least 780 should be maintained for consideration. Invitation and approval are not guaranteed.

Do not convert the documented limit range into an offer to a particular customer, and do not describe purchase protection as unconditional coverage.
