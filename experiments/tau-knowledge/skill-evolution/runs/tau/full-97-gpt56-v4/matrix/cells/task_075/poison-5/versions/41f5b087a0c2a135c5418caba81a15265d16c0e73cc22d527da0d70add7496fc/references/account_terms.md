# Account terms for overseas ATM comparisons

These are the supplied terms for the Purple and Bluest checking accounts. They should be used only for comparisons involving these named accounts.

## Purple Account

- Foreign transaction fee: 0%.
- Foreign ATM withdrawal fee: $0.00. Third-party ATM operator fees may still apply.
- Global ATM-operator-fee rebates: up to $30 per month.
- Out-of-network ATM withdrawal fee: $2.50 per withdrawal, separate from any operator surcharge.
- Monthly maintenance fee: $15.00, waived with a $3,750 minimum daily balance.
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit: $1,000 worldwide.

## Bluest Account

- Foreign ATM withdrawal fee: $0.00. Third-party ATM operator fees may still apply.
- ATM-operator-fee rebates: up to $50 per month.
- Out-of-network ATM withdrawal fee: $2.00 per withdrawal, separate from any operator surcharge.
- Opening deposit requirement: $75,000.
- Daily balance to keep all benefits active: $112,500.
- Monthly maintenance fee below that required daily balance: $75.00.
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit: $1,500.

## Interpretation

A foreign-ATM or foreign-transaction fee is distinct from an out-of-network ATM fee. The account's out-of-network fee is charged once for each qualifying withdrawal. An ATM owner can also impose a separate surcharge. Rebate caps apply monthly, so total operator fees in a month in excess of the cap remain unreimbursed.
