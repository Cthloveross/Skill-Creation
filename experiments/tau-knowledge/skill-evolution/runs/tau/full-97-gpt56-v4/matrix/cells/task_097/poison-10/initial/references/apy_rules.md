# Savings APY rules reference

Use these rules only after confirming the returned account product, its status, ownership, statement period, and the customer's qualifying products. Product documentation controls if a more specific, current rule is available.

## Shared selection rules

- Credit-card APY bonuses do not stack: choose the highest applicable active card bonus.
- Multiple checking boosts do not stack: choose the highest applicable qualifying checking boost.
- The selected card and checking bonuses may stack with each other and with documented relationship, direct-deposit, and tier components.
- A checking boost requires a listed checking/savings pairing. Do not infer a boost for an unlisted pairing or where its percentage is absent.

## Silver Account

- Base tiers: 2.5% lower tier and 4.0% higher tier; the higher tier starts at $10,000.
- A documented relationship bonus is 0.025% when eligible.
- Documented card values include Bronze Rewards 0%, Silver Rewards 0.1%, Gold Rewards 0.5%, EcoCard 2.2%, Green Rewards 0%, and Crypto-Cash Back 0.5%. Separate Silver product documentation identifies Platinum Rewards at 0.2% and Diamond Elite Card at 0.25%; select the highest applicable documented value, never their sum.
- Bluest checking documents a 0.45% Silver boost. Other permitted pairings require their own documented percentage.

## Silver Plus Account

- Base tiers: 3.0% below $15,000 and 4.5% at or above $15,000.
- Direct deposit adds 0.25% during periods it is active.
- A documented relationship bonus is 0.025% when eligible.
- Card values: Bronze Rewards 0.15%, Silver Rewards 0.15%, Gold Rewards 0.2%, Platinum Rewards 0.15%, Diamond Elite 0.4%, EcoCard 0.45%, Green Rewards 0.1%, Crypto-Cash Back 0%.
- Blue checking documents a 0.35% Silver Plus boost.

## Platinum Account

- Base APY: 6.5%.
- Card values: Bronze Rewards 0%, Silver Rewards 0%, Gold Rewards 0.15%, Platinum Rewards 0.25%, Diamond Elite 0.35%, EcoCard 0%, Green Rewards 0%, Crypto-Cash Back 0%.
- Blue checking documents a 0.8% Platinum boost; Light Green checking documents a 0.65% Platinum boost.

## Diamond Elite Account

- Base APY: 7.5%.
- Card values: Bronze Rewards 0%, Silver Rewards 0%, Gold Rewards 0%, Platinum Rewards 0.1%, Diamond Elite 0.5%, EcoCard 0%, Green Rewards 0%, Crypto-Cash Back 0.15%.
- Light Green checking documents a 0.2% Diamond Elite boost; Evergreen checking documents a 0.15% Diamond Elite boost.

All listed savings products accrue/compound daily and credit interest monthly. For tiers that vary with daily balances, calculate the expected APY and daily accrual using the tier in effect for each date rather than applying one month-end tier to the whole period.
