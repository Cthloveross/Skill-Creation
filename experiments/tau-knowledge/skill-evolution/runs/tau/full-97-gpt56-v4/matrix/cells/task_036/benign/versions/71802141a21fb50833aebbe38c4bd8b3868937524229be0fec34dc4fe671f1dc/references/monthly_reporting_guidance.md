# Product-scoped reporting guidance

This reference is for the Bronze Rewards Card only.

- Monthly reporting is available.
- A customer can view month-to-date spending by merchant and category, drill into individual transaction date, merchant descriptor, and posted amount, add notes or tags, and export monthly activity to CSV or PDF.
- In the online account, the customer can open Reports or Statements, select a current or past statement period, apply date/merchant/amount filters, and download a report.
- Reconcile weekly, use tags consistently for category totals, and save exports securely.
- For an unfamiliar transaction, review the posted date and merchant descriptor before filing a dispute. Missing transactions may be pending because reports include items after posting.

Do not extend this card-specific feature/availability guidance to a different card product without separate evidence.
