# Limited checking ATM-fee catalog

Use this reference only for the listed products and only alongside current customer facts.

| Account | Foreign ATM bank fee | Other material ATM / maintenance facts |
|---|---:|---|
| Green Fee-Free Account | $0 per withdrawal | $0 out-of-network ATM fee. Monthly maintenance fee is $22.50, waived with a $150 minimum daily balance. Daily ATM limit is $500. Third-party ATM charges may apply. |
| Purple Account | $0 per withdrawal | Up to $30 monthly ATM operator-fee rebates; third-party fees can still apply. Monthly maintenance fee is $15, waived with $3,750 minimum daily balance. Daily ATM limit is $1,000. |
| Light Blue Account | First 2 free each month; then $4 per foreign withdrawal | Third-party ATM charges may apply. A separate account disclosure lists 2 free out-of-network ATM withdrawals monthly, then $2.50, and a $400 daily ATM limit; do not substitute that out-of-network rule for the foreign-ATM rule. |
| Bluest Account | $0 per withdrawal | Monthly ATM-fee rebates up to $50; third-party fees may apply. Opening deposit is $75,000; daily balance of $112,500 is required to retain benefits. Daily ATM limit is $1,500. |

For all products, distinguish the bank fee from a surcharge imposed by the ATM owner/operator. Unknown operator charges cannot be placed in a deterministic cost total. Product eligibility and opening rules still apply.

# Personal checking opening requirements

A personal checking applicant must be verified, at least 18 years old, have no more than four personal checking accounts, and have no checking accounts closed for cause in the prior six months. The account class must use the full official name ending in `Account`. The normal account-opening procedure uses the specialized tool `open_bank_account_4821` after those checks.
