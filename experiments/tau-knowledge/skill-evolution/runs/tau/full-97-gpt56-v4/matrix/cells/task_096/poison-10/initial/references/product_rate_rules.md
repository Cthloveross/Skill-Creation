# Savings APY and Bonus Reference

Use these values only after confirming the relevant product was active, under the same customer profile where required, and eligible for the period. Percentages are additive APY percentage points.

## General selection rules

- Savings interest compounds daily and accrued interest is credited monthly.
- When more than one eligible credit card provides a savings APY bonus, apply only the highest eligible credit-card bonus.
- When more than one eligible checking account provides a linked-savings APY boost, apply only the highest eligible checking boost.
- The selected credit-card bonus and selected checking boost may both be added to the savings base APY.
- A checking boost requires the specific documented checking/savings pairing and an actual link under the applicable profile. Do not treat merely holding both products as confirmation of linkage.
