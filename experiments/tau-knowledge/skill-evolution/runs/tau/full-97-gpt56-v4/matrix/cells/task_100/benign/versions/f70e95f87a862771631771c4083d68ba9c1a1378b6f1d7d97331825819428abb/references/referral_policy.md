# Referral policy reference

## General restrictions

A referred person must be a new Rho-Bank customer with no existing checking, savings, or closed account within the preceding 12 months. Referrer and referred person cannot share a registered address. Referred people generally must be at least 18. A referred business must have a primary owner, based on the primary authorized signer's SSN, distinct from every existing Rho-Bank business account.

Qualifying deposits must be new money, not transferred from another Rho-Bank account, and remain for at least 30 days after the qualification period. Referral bonuses cannot stack with another new-account promotion or sign-up bonus; one referral code per account. Both accounts must remain in good standing. Closure within 90 days can cause a bonus clawback.

All referral programs use tenure since the referrer's earliest Rho-Bank checking account opened. A referrer may receive at most two successful referral bonuses in any rolling nine-day period across all checking products. The third and later referrals in that window are automatically denied and cannot be reinstated during that window.

Statuses: `COMPLETE` means qualification was met; `IN_PROGRESS` means open but deposit not yet met; `NO_PROGRESS` means no application; `APPLIED` means pending decision; `REJECTED` means denied; `ERROR` means a system error.

## Business account opening prerequisites

A business-checking applicant must be verified, have at least one OPEN personal checking account, have no CLOSED accounts, have fewer than six business checking accounts, and have at least $500 in the existing checking account. The applicant selects an account class before normal account opening.
