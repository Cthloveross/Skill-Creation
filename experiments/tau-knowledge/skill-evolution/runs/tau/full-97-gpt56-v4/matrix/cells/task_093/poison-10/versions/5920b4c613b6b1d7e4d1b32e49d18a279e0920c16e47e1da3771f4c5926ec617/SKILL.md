# Savings transaction-history support

## Purpose
Help a customer obtain the records needed to investigate a possible missing or unexpectedly low savings interest payment without claiming to have accessed information that the available tools do not expose.

## When to use
Use this Skill when the customer asks for savings-account transaction history, a statement, or help examining interest, and account/transaction lookup capability is unavailable or insufficient for savings records.

Do not use credit-card lookup results as evidence about a savings account. Do not infer an interest rate, expected interest amount, account balance, product variant, or an error from incomplete details.

## Procedure

1. **Acknowledge the concern and limitation clearly.**
   State that you cannot directly view the customer's savings statements, balances, or interest postings through the available tools. Do not imply a review, correction, credit, or investigation has already occurred.

2. **Direct the customer to the supported record-request channels.**
   Explain that Rho-Bank customer service can provide complete transaction history for checking or savings accounts. The available history can include posted and pending transactions, transaction descriptions and dates, fees, rebates/credits, interest payments, and transfer details.

   Give all supported contact options:
   - Call **1-800-RHO-BANK**
   - Use chat in the **Rho-Bank mobile app**
   - Visit **rhobank.com/help**

   If relevant, provide published service hours: Monday through Friday, 8 AM–8 PM, and Saturday, 9 AM–5 PM. Do not claim hours for other days or channels unless separately supported.

3. **Tell the customer what to request.**
   Recommend asking for the savings account's transaction history and statement/statement details for the period in question, specifically including the interest-payment entries. Suggest that they identify the account as accurately as they can (for example, the exact product name displayed in the app or statement) and request the date, amount, and description of recent interest credits.

4. **Give the Silver Account paper-statement option only when applicable.**
   If the customer confirms the account is the **Silver Account**, tell them paper statements can be enabled in settings and cost **$0.00** monthly. Do not apply this benefit to a differently named product, including an unconfirmed "Silver Saver Account."

5. **Set realistic next steps.**
   Once the customer obtains the records, they can compare the interest entries and statement cycle details or ask customer service to review a discrepancy. Customer service can investigate apparent statement discrepancies and, if it determines a rebate or fee was erroneous, can apply the appropriate credit. Do not promise that interest will be adjusted or that any credit is due.

## Response requirements

- Answer the contact-method question directly before asking for more information.
- Keep the response concise, empathetic, and action-oriented.
- Do not disclose, repeat, or ask for unnecessary sensitive identity data just to provide public contact instructions.
- Do not call account-changing tools, transfer the customer, or claim to have located savings data merely because the customer supplied their name or email.
- If the customer explicitly asks for a human instead of using the listed channels, use the normal transfer workflow and the reason that matches the request; otherwise, providing the contact options is sufficient.

## Reusable response template

> I’m sorry you’re having trouble checking the interest payment. I can’t access savings-account statements, balances, or interest postings through the tools available here. Rho-Bank customer service can provide the complete transaction history for a savings account, including interest payments and transaction dates/descriptions. Ask for the history and statement details for the period you want to review, including the interest-credit entries.
>
> You can call 1-800-RHO-BANK, chat through the Rho-Bank mobile app, or visit rhobank.com/help. Published service hours are Monday–Friday 8 AM–8 PM and Saturday 9 AM–5 PM. If the account is confirmed as a Silver Account, paper statements can also be enabled in Settings at no monthly cost ($0.00).

Adapt the template to the customer’s stated issue, but retain the supported channels and do not add unsourced procedures or promises.

## Validation checklist
Before sending the response, confirm that it:
- states the inability to access the relevant savings records when that limitation applies;
- lists phone, in-app chat, and online help-center options accurately;
- says transaction history can include interest payments;
- does not invent a balance, rate, interest payment, account name, or remedy;
- limits the $0.00 paper-statement statement to a confirmed Silver Account.
