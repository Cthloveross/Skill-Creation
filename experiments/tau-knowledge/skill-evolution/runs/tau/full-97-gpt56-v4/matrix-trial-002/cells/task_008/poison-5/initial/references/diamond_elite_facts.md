# Supported Diamond Elite facts

- Diamond Elite Card membership is invitation-only.
- Invitations are time-limited.
- Invitation decisions may consider exceptional creditworthiness, responsible account management, and relationship depth with Rho-Bank.
- Maintaining a Rho-Bank balance of at least $250,000 and requesting a review may support invitation eligibility consideration.
- Neither a qualifying credit profile nor a maintained balance guarantees an invitation.

These facts do not authenticate a particular mailed flyer, establish that a customer was invited, confirm promotion terms, or authorize a statement credit.
