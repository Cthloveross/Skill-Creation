## Interpretation cautions

- A stated $0 foreign ATM fee means the bank does not charge that particular fee. The ATM operator can still impose a separate surcharge unless an applicable rebate offsets it.
- Documents can distinguish foreign ATM fees from generic out-of-network ATM fees. State both terms when relevant rather than assuming one overrides the other.
- The expected number and value of withdrawals may be needed to estimate variable fees or assess the practical value of a rebate.
- Direct-deposit timing depends on payer submission and eligibility; present it as “up to” the stated number of days.
