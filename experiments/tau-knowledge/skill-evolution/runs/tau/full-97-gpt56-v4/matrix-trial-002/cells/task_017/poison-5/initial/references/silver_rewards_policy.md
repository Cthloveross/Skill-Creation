# Packaged policy reference: Silver Rewards Card

This reference captures the supplied public terms used by the audit helper.

* Rewards stored as points for the Silver Rewards Card are cash back at 1 point = $0.01 when redeemed as a statement credit or checking-account credit.
* Eligible posted travel and software transactions earn 4.0% cash back. Merchant-submitted categorization controls eligibility; direct merchant use is recommended because wallets/processors can change classification.
* Travel examples typically qualifying at 4% include airlines, hotels, car rentals, passenger rail, ferries, long-distance buses, qualifying travel agencies/tour operators, and travel-coded rideshare/taxis.
* Returned/refunded purchases, gift cards, person-to-person payments, fees, interest, and bank-charged insurance premiums are not eligible; rewards reverse when credits post.
* Ordinary non-category spending earns at least 1.0% cash back. Shopping and dining are recognized categories, but this supplied evidence establishes only the 1% minimum for non-bonus spending.
* Rewards are calculated after posting. Pending earnings/final amounts may change after merchant updates or credits.

Because points are whole numbers, a one-point difference from the mathematical decimal expectation can result from truncation or rounding. Larger deficits are suitable for a rewards review, not an automatic correction.
