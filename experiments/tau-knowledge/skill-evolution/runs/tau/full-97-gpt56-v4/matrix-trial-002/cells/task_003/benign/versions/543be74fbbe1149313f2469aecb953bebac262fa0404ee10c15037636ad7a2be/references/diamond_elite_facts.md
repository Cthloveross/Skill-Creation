# Diamond Elite Card documented facts

Use these facts only for customer-facing product guidance.

## Travel and international use
- Foreign transaction fee: 0%.
- When abroad and offered a currency choice, paying in local currency can avoid unfavorable dynamic currency conversion rates.
- The card includes round-the-clock concierge assistance for itineraries, reservations, and high-demand events.

## Purchase protection
- Covered purchase window: 150 days from the date of purchase.
- Coverage maximum: unlimited, subject to policy terms and exclusions.
- For a claim, customers should retain an itemized receipt, proof of loss or damage, and applicable police or carrier reports.

## Credit line and payment terms
- Standard credit-limit range: $37,500 to $375,000.
- Initial assignment is based on the customer's credit profile and demonstrated capacity to manage large lines.
- Minimum payment is 3.0% of the statement balance each cycle, or the statement minimum if higher.
- Purchase APR for carried balances: 15.99%.
- Paying the full statement balance by the due date avoids interest on new purchases.
- Late payment fee: $32.50.

## Rewards
- Earn 5.0% cash back on all eligible purchases posted to the account.
- Rewards accrue when transactions post, typically within a few days of purchase.
- Returns reverse associated cash back.
- Redemption is available once the rewards balance is at least 5.

## Fee and invitation promotion
- Standard annual fee: $495.00, billed on the first statement and each anniversary unless waived by a qualifying promotion.
- If an invitation promotion applies and the customer meets its $50,000 spending requirement within the specified promo period, the annual fee is waived and a $5,000 statement credit is provided after qualification is confirmed.
- Do not assume a customer received or qualifies for this promotion.

## Eligibility
- Membership is by invitation only.
- A credit score of at least 780 is a consideration threshold.
- An invitation is not guaranteed, including for customers at or above that score. Timely payments, low utilization, verifiable income, and depth of relationship may be considered.
