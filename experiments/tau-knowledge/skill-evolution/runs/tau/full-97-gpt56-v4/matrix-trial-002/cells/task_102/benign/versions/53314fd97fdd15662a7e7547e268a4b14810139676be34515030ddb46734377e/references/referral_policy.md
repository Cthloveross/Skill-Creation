# Checking referral policy reference

## Shared requirements and restrictions

- A referrer must meet the relevant tenure threshold, measured from the date their **earliest Rho-Bank checking account** was opened. Their current account type does not control this calculation, and they may refer to a different checking product if the tenure requirement is met.
- The referrer and referred account must remain in good standing for bonuses to be paid.
- A referred person must be a new Rho-Bank customer: no existing checking or savings account and no closed account within the past 12 months.
- A referrer and referred person cannot be registered at the same address.
- A referred business must have a different primary owner from every existing Rho-Bank business account, compared using the primary authorized signer's SSN. This is a secure back-office comparison; do not ask for or disclose an SSN in chat.
- The qualifying deposit must be new money, not a transfer from another Rho-Bank account, and must remain in the account for at least 30 days after the qualifying period ends.
- A referral bonus cannot be combined with another new-account promotion or sign-up bonus, and only one referral code can be applied to a new account.
- If the referred account closes within 90 days of opening, a paid referral bonus may be clawed back.
- Across every checking account type, the referrer can receive at most **two referral bonuses in a rolling nine-day window**. The calculation uses exact timestamps of recent successful bonuses, not calendar weeks. A third or later referral in the window is automatically denied and cannot be reinstated during that window.

## Referral statuses

- `COMPLETE`: account opened and criteria met for the referral bonus.
- `IN_PROGRESS`: account opened; qualifying deposit requirement remains.
- `NO_PROGRESS`: no application yet.
- `APPLIED`: application submitted and awaiting decision.
- `REJECTED`: denied, such as for a cap or eligibility restriction.
- `ERROR`: system error; retry later or contact support.

## Product-specific referral requirements

### Gold Years Account

- Referred primary holder must be age 62 or older.
- Referrer tenure: 30 days.
- Deposit: at least $1,000 within 90 days of opening.
- Annual maximum: 6 referral bonuses.
- Referrer bonus: $50; referred welcome bonus: $75.

### Dark Green Account

- Referrer tenure: 45 days.
- Deposit: at least $1,000 within 60 days of opening.
- Annual maximum: 6 referral bonuses.
- Referrer bonus: $40; referred welcome bonus: $30.
- The account itself is for primary holders ages 17 through 26; confirm account eligibility separately where relevant.

### Light Green Account

- The account is for primary holders ages 13 through 24.
- The general rule permits minors for Light Green with a guardian.
- No Light Green referral-bonus amount, tenure, deposit window, or annual cap is established in this reference. Do not invent any of those terms; obtain the product referral terms before recommending or confirming qualification.

### Sky Blue Account

- Intended for companies within four years of formation.
- Referrer tenure: 45 days.
- Referred startup deposit: at least $10,000 within 90 days of account opening.
- Annual maximum: 8 referrals.
- Referrer bonus: $150; referred welcome bonus: $250.

### Lime Green Account

- Referrer tenure: 90 days.
- Referred business deposit: at least $15,000 within 90 days.
- Annual maximum: 12 referrals.
- Referrer bonus: $200; referred-business welcome bonus: $150.

The product conditions above are cumulative with all shared requirements.
