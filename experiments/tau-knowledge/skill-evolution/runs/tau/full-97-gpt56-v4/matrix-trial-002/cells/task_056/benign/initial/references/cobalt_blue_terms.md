# Cobalt Blue documented terms

Use only where applicable to the customer's stated needs.

- Cobalt Blue does not assess overdraft fees.
- Monthly maintenance fee: $20. It can be waived by maintaining a daily balance at or above $2,500 during the statement period.
- The account includes 175 free monthly transactions (deposits, withdrawals, and transfers). Additional transactions may incur a fee; no amount is documented here.
- Eligible debit-card purchases earn 1.0% cashback. Cashback is calculated on the net purchase amount after returns or adjustments, accrues as purchases settle, and reverses when the related purchase is reversed or refunded.
- Up to six business debit cards may be requested for team members.
- Cobalt Blue provides up to $15 per month in out-of-network ATM-fee rebates. The rebate cap is monthly. Rebates are applied after the ATM operator fee posts; customers should keep receipts until the rebate appears in activity.
- Domestic out-of-network ATM withdrawal: the bank-assessed fee is $2.00 per transaction. ATM owners may assess a separate surcharge. International ATM withdrawal: 2.5% of the withdrawal amount, subject to a $4.00 minimum. Exchange rates, conversion choices, and local surcharges can affect total cost.
- Mobile check deposit daily limit: $10,000.
- Standard ACH transfers are available with no fee; same-day ACH is listed at $0.00, subject to submission and approval cutoffs. Outgoing domestic wires cost $20.00.

Do not promise an account conversion or opening procedure from these terms alone. Do not extend Cobalt Blue terms to another account class.
