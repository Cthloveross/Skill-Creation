# Product comparison notes

Use these notes only together with the current product disclosures and authoritative customer account records.

## Known linked-checking pairings

The published pairing list includes the following relevant examples:

- Green Account (checking) with Gold Account (savings)
- Green Fee-Free Account (checking) with Gold Plus Account (savings)
- Evergreen Account (checking) with Green Account (savings)
- Blue Account (checking) with Silver Plus Account (savings)
- Green Account (checking) with Silver Account (savings)
- Blue Account (checking) with Platinum Account (savings)
- Purple Account (checking) with Gold Account (savings)
- Bluest Account (checking) with Silver Account or Bronze Account (savings)

Other listed pairings may be relevant. A pairing not documented as qualifying must not be represented as a boost.

Known disclosure examples give Blue checking a +0.35% Silver Plus boost and Evergreen checking a +0.55% Green savings boost. Product documents may specify more exact boosts and fees; use the current disclosure for the actual recommendation.

## Non-stacking rules

- Multiple qualifying checking boosts do not stack: apply the highest single applicable checking boost.
- Multiple qualifying credit-card bonuses do not stack: apply the highest single applicable card bonus.
- The selected checking boost may combine with a qualifying card bonus or another separately documented relationship bonus, subject to all conditions.

## Balance-sensitive examples

- Silver Plus has a $1,000 opening deposit, $2,500 ongoing minimum, and a $15,000 Tier 2 threshold. Its published base tiers are 3.0% below the Tier 2 threshold and 4.5% at or above it; an $8 monthly fee may apply below $2,500.
- Green savings has a $500 minimum balance, no monthly maintenance fee, and a published 4.0% APY.
- Gold savings has a published 5.5% APY and a $10,000 minimum balance; an active Gold Rewards Card may reduce that minimum to $5,000, but do not assume the card is held.

Quote requirements and fees accurately, and distinguish the rate on savings from the economics of holding the linked checking account.
