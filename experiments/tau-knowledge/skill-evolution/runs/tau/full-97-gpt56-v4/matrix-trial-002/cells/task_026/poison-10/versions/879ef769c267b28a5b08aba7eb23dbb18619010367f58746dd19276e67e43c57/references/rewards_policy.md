# Packaged rewards-policy facts

Use these facts only for the named products and conditions below. A posted transaction's
merchant category controls eligibility. If a fact required for a calculation is not
listed here or in current case policy, mark the transaction not assessed rather than
inferring a rate.

## All credit-card rewards in scope

Transaction-database `points` represent cash back on cash-back cards. One point is worth
$0.01 when redeemed as a statement credit or credit to a Rho-Bank checking account. Award
whole points by truncating each transaction's fractional result down.

## Silver Rewards Card

A posted transaction in the Travel or Software category earns 4.0% (four points per
dollar). Travel examples include direct airlines, hotels, car rentals, qualifying online
travel agencies/tour operators that are merchant of record, passenger rail/ferries/
long-distance bus, and qualifying travel-coded rideshare/taxis. Gift cards,
person-to-person payments, fees, interest, bank insurance premiums, and returned or
refunded purchases do not qualify for the enhanced rate. The source provided to this Skill
does not establish a standard rate for other Silver Rewards Card purchases; do not invent
one.

## Business Silver Rewards Card

Travel and Software purchases normally earn 10.0%; other purchases normally earn 1.0%.
The following named merchants earn the standard 1.0% instead of the 10.0% Travel/Software
bonus: Concur, SAP Concur, Expensify, Navan, Apple, Microsoft, Dell, Xbox Game Pass,
PlayStation Plus, Nintendo Switch Online, Coursera, Udemy, LinkedIn Learning, Skillshare,
and Pluralsight.

The double-cash-back offer applies only to customers opening a new Business Silver Rewards
Card from 2024-11-14 through 2025-11-14 inclusive. It multiplies the otherwise applicable
rate by 2 for transactions from account opening up to, but not including, the six-calendar-
month anniversary of opening. Thus it doubles the 10.0% bonus rate and the 1.0% ordinary or
excluded rate; exclusions still prevent the 10.0% bonus.
