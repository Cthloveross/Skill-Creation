# Product evidence for business-checking recommendations

Use these facts only in the account-recommendation context for which the associated product documents apply. A minimum balance requirement and a fee-waiver balance are different conditions and must be described separately.

## Lime Green

- Overdraft fee: $0.00.
- Minimum balance requirement: $5,000.
- Monthly maintenance fee: $25.00. The fee is waived when the defined account balance meets or exceeds $15,000; a balance below that during the evaluation period may result in the fee.
- Eligible out-of-network ATM-fee rebates: up to $25 per month. Domestic out-of-network transactions are assessed $1.00; international ATM withdrawals are assessed 1.5% of the withdrawal amount, with a $2.50 minimum.
- Service perk: dedicated account-manager support. Direct messages receive a response within two business hours; the priority-line maximum expected wait is six minutes.

## Sky Blue

- An applicant company must be within four years of formation and meet the stated funding requirement of zero.
- It has a six-month free period; afterward its monthly maintenance fee is $25.00.
- Overdraft fee: $0.00.
- Monthly out-of-network ATM-fee rebates: up to $15.
- Its formation-date condition must be confirmed from legal formation documents, such as a formation certificate or articles of organization. If the date is unavailable, eligibility cannot be confirmed.

## Promotional priority

From November 1 through November 30, 2025, when multiple accounts meet **all** stated customer requirements, recommend in this order:

1. Sky Blue;
2. Lime Green;
3. another qualifying account only if neither Sky Blue nor Lime Green meets the requirements.

The promotional order never permits recommending an account that does not meet every stated requirement. An account with an unconfirmed required eligibility condition is not a confirmed qualifying recommendation.

## Account-opening boundary

An eventual business-checking opening requires a verified customer, at least one existing OPEN personal checking account, no more than six business checking accounts, no CLOSED accounts, and an existing checking-account balance of at least $500. The desired account class must be confirmed before the normal account-opening procedure is used. These are not prerequisites for providing an informational product recommendation.
