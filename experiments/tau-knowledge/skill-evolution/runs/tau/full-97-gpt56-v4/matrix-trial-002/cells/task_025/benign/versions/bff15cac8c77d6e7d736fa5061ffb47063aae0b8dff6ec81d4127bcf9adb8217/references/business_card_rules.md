# Business card product rules for large-purchase comparisons

Use these as source facts and retain the merchant-coding qualifications stated below.

## Business Silver Rewards Card

- Credit limits generally range from $17,500 to $112,500, subject to credit review and business financials.
- Eligible travel and software purchases earn 10.0%; other purchases earn 1.0%. The transaction must process under a qualifying travel or software merchant category.
- Typical software examples are SaaS, enterprise applications, cloud infrastructure, developer tools, cybersecurity, and marketing/analytics platforms. Direct/provider billing and software coding matter.
- Named exclusions from the 10% rate include Apple, Microsoft, and Dell as hardware/electronics merchants. This identifies a hardware/electronics exception, not the category or rate for every service offered by those brands.
- Standard annual fee is $122.50 after the first year. Product materials state that new customers receive a first-year annual fee of $0, including the approval/pricing terms and the fee-waiver terms. A separately dated new-customer waiver runs from 2025-11-15 through 2026-01-15; it must not be used to negate the stated general first-year new-customer fee when that general term applies. Confirm the account disclosures if its new-customer status or fee term is uncertain.
- A separate double-cash-back offer runs 2024-11-14 through 2025-11-14. New customers opening during the offer receive twice the normal rate for the first six months after opening: qualifying travel/software is multiplied from 10% to 20%, and other purchases from 1% to 2%. Merchant exclusions continue to apply.
- Minimum personal credit score is 700; established businesses may qualify based on PAYDEX of at least 47, while newer businesses may be considered with strong personal credit.

## Business Platinum Rewards Card

- Credit limits range from $75,000 to $400,000, subject to approval.
- Travel, software, and media advertising purchases earn 4.0%; all other purchases earn 1.5%. Merchant coding controls the rate.
- Qualifying media/advertising examples are digital advertising, search and social ads, display/retargeting, and streaming audio/video media placements when billed by a media network or advertising platform. This does not state that an ordinary consumer or business streaming subscription earns the media-advertising rate.
- A new-customer first-year annual-fee waiver applies to accounts opened from 2025-11-01 through 2026-02-28. The usual annual fee is $450.00.
- Underwriting requirements include personal credit score 765 and PAYDEX 77 for established businesses.

## Business Gold Rewards Card

- Credit limits start at $37,500 and may reach $225,000, subject to approval and review.
- Eligible operations purchases earn 2.5%; all others earn 1.0%. Merchant category coding controls the rate.
- Operations examples include business utilities and telecom, cloud services and software subscriptions used for daily operations, shipping/logistics, office supplies/maintenance, and bookkeeping or payroll administration. Entertainment, personal, and non-business coded transactions are listed as non-qualifying examples.
- Annual fee is $200.00. Underwriting requires personal credit score 735 and PAYDEX 67.

## Category and representation principles

- Streaming services are described as entertainment. Software is separately described as subscriptions, licenses, and cloud services.
- Cash-back-card database rewards may be labelled points, but one point represents $0.01 in cash back. Therefore 1% of a $100 purchase is $1.00 (100 points), and percentage calculations may be presented in cash dollars.
- Returns and credits reduce net purchases and associated rewards.
