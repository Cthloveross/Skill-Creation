# Published personal-card comparison facts

Use these as product-level facts, not as customer-specific eligibility or approval decisions.

## Gold Rewards Card

- Cash back: 2.5% on all purchases.
- Annual fee: $0.00.
- Minimum credit score: 720.
- An active Rho-Bank+ premium subscription is required.
- APR on carried balances: 17.99%; foreign transaction fee: 0%.
- Rewards redemption minimum: $15.

## Platinum Rewards Card

- Cash back: 10.0% on eligible purchases. Eligible purchases are posted point-of-sale and online purchases; cash advances, balance transfers, fees, interest, and cash-equivalent transactions are excluded.
- Returns or credits reduce previously earned rewards.
- Standard annual fee: $200.00.
- Stated minimum credit score: 750.
- APR on carried balances: 16.99% variable; foreign transaction fee: 0%.
- A first-year annual-fee waiver promotion applied only to applications submitted and approved in the 2024-06-01 through 2024-12-31 promo window. The standard $200 fee applies after the first anniversary. Do not represent this expired window as a current offer.
- A separate annual-fee rebate may require meeting an unspecified monthly spending threshold in every one of 12 account-anniversary windows. The published material supplied here does not provide the threshold amount, so do not calculate or promise that rebate.

## Diamond Elite Card

- Cash back: 5.0% on eligible purchases.
- Standard annual fee: $495.00.
- Membership is by invitation only. A score of at least 780 is indicated for consideration, but neither that score nor any other stated factor guarantees an invitation.
- The $495 fee can be waived only under the stated invitation promotion after $50,000 of spending within its specified promo period; the provided terms do not establish that the promotion applies to a particular customer.
- APR on carried balances: 15.99%; foreign transaction fee: 0%.

## General interpretation

Reward values are estimates based on posted eligible purchases. Returns, refunds, credits, and excluded transaction types can reduce the result. Annual-fee comparisons do not include interest, late fees, merchant pricing, tax effects, subscription cost, or benefits not quantified in the published terms.
