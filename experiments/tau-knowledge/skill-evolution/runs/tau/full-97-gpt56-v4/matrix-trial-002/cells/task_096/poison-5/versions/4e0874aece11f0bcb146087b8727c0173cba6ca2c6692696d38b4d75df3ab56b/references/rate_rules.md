# Supported savings interest rules

## Processing

- Bronze Savings interest compounds daily and is credited monthly as a single interest credit.
- Gold Plus interest compounds daily and is credited monthly.
- Balance fluctuations and a first partial period affect the amount credited.

## Base APYs

| Savings account | Base APY |
|---|---:|
| Bronze Savings | 2.0% |
| Gold Plus Savings | 6.0% |

Bronze has no monthly maintenance fee.

## Qualifying linked-checking APY boosts in the supplied materials

A boost applies only for the named checking/savings pairing under the same customer profile. These boosts are additive to base APY and any eligible card bonus.

| Checking account | Savings account | APY boost |
|---|---|---:|
| Bluest Account | Bronze Savings | +0.7% |
| Green Fee-Free Account | Bronze Savings | +0.4% |
| Gold Years Account | Gold Plus Savings | +0.5% |

The supplied rules say only specified pairs qualify. They do not say that multiple qualifying checking boosts may be stacked on the same savings account.

## Gold Plus eligible credit-card APY bonuses

These require the named card and Gold Plus account under the same customer profile.

| Card | APY bonus |
|---|---:|
| Bronze Rewards Card | +0.15% |
| Silver Rewards Card | +0.1% |
| Gold Rewards Card | +0.35% |
| Platinum Rewards Card | +0.2% |
| Diamond Elite Card | +0.25% |
| EcoCard | +0.1% |
| Green Rewards Card | +0.05% |
| Crypto-Cash Back Card | +0.3% |

Do not infer card ownership or eligibility from a statement amount.
