# Documented savings APY rules

Use only for the named products below. These are product rules, not proof that a particular customer currently qualifies.

## Shared selection rules

- Base savings APY, one highest eligible linked-checking boost, and one highest eligible card bonus are additive.
- When more than one eligible linked checking account exists, select only the highest applicable checking boost.
- When more than one eligible card exists, select only the highest applicable card bonus.
- Checking boosts do not stack with other checking boosts. Card bonuses do not stack with other card bonuses. The selected checking boost may combine with the selected card bonus.
- Interest compounds daily and is credited monthly for Bronze and Gold Plus.

## Bronze Savings

- Base APY: 2.0%.
- Documented qualifying checking relationships relevant to these rules:
  - Bluest checking linked to Bronze Savings: +0.70%.
  - Green Fee-Free checking linked to Bronze Savings: +0.40%.
- Documented Bronze card bonuses:
  - Bronze Rewards Card: +0.10%.
  - Silver Rewards Card: +0.00%.
  - Gold Rewards Card: +0.25%.
  - EcoCard: +0.00%.
  - Green Rewards Card: +0.20%.
  - Crypto-Cash Back Card: +0.30%.
- No monthly maintenance fee. Balance changes and a mid-period opening affect the interest credit.

## Gold Plus Savings

- Base APY: 6.0% on eligible balances.
- Documented qualifying checking relationships relevant to these rules:
  - Gold Years checking linked to Gold Plus Savings: +0.50%.
  - Green Fee-Free checking linked to Gold Plus Savings: +0.35%.
- Documented Gold Plus card bonuses:
  - Bronze Rewards Card: +0.15%.
  - Silver Rewards Card: +0.10%.
  - Gold Rewards Card: +0.35%.
  - Platinum Rewards Card: +0.20%.
  - Diamond Elite Card: +0.25%.
  - EcoCard: +0.10%.
  - Green Rewards Card: +0.05%.
  - Crypto-Cash Back Card: +0.30%.
- Gold Plus has a $25,000 minimum balance requirement. Its automatic investment sweep may move excess funds to a linked investment account when the end-of-day available balance exceeds $50,000, according to sweep settings. This can reduce the daily eligible balance used for interest.

## Exact-reconciliation evidence

For an exact result, obtain the statement-cycle start/end dates, daily eligible balance history, active linkage status for each claimed checking boost, active same-profile status for each claimed card bonus, the actual posted interest transaction, and the ledger's disclosed rate-conversion method. Do not invent missing daily balances or linkage facts.
