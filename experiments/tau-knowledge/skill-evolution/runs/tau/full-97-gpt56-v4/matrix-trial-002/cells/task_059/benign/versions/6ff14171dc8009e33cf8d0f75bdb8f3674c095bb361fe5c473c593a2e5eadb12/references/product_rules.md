# Product rules for savings-and-card comparison

Use only terms relevant to the customer's scenario. Product eligibility remains subject to actual application/underwriting and account linkage where applicable.

## Bronze Savings Account

- Base APY: **2.0%**.
- Minimum opening deposit and minimum balance: **$0**.
- Monthly maintenance fee: **$0**.
- Interest compounds daily and is credited monthly. APY is the annual effective yield for an annual estimate.
- Bronze card-linked APY bonuses:
  - Bronze Rewards: +0.1%
  - Silver Rewards: +0%
  - Gold Rewards: +0.25%
  - EcoCard: +0%
  - Green Rewards: +0.2%
  - Crypto-Cash Back: +0.3%
- Standard free-withdrawal allowance is six per month. Excess withdrawals and paper statements can have separate fees; they are not monthly maintenance fees.

## Gold Account

- Base APY: **5.5%**.
- Standard minimum balance: **$10,000**.
- Gold Rewards Card holders have a reduced account minimum of **$5,000** and a stated +0.025% relationship/APY benefit. Arithmetic from those stated values is 5.525% before other permitted bonuses.
- The source that describes this benefit also labels 5.5% + 0.025% as 6.0%, which is arithmetically inconsistent. Do not use 6.0% without clarification.
- Gold Account card APY-bonus table: Bronze +0.15%, Silver +0.2%, Gold +0.025%, Platinum +0.15%, Diamond +0.3%, Eco +0.6%, Green +0.35%, Crypto-Cash Back +0%.
- A card-linked benefit applies when the savings account and card are under the same customer profile.

## Other savings information that may prevent a reliable ranking

- Silver Account has balance-tier APYs of either 2.5% or 4.0%, daily compounding, and monthly interest crediting. The tier thresholds are not supplied here, so do not assume which rate applies to a given balance.
- Silver Account card APY bonuses include Bronze +0%, Silver +0.1%, Gold +0.5%, Eco +2.2%, Green +0%, and Crypto-Cash Back +0.5%.

## Checking-to-savings boosts

Only explicitly listed checking/savings pairs qualify. A customer holding **Light Blue checking** has no documented qualifying pairing in the supplied pair list and therefore should not receive a checking-linked APY boost based on that account.

If multiple qualifying checking accounts exist, only the highest applicable checking boost applies. If multiple cards provide card APY bonuses, only the highest applicable card bonus applies. A qualifying checking boost and the selected qualifying card bonus may be additive where documented.

## Card comparison facts

### Bronze Rewards Card

- Minimum credit score: **640**.
- Annual fee: **$0**.
- General eligible-purchase cash back: **1.0%**.
- Bronze Savings APY bonus: +0.1%.
- Standard carried-balance APR is 20.49%; introductory APR is 0% for the first year. Do not treat an APR as a savings yield.

### Silver Rewards Card

- Minimum credit score: **680**.
- Annual fee: **$0**.
- Earns **4.0%** on eligible travel and software/SaaS transactions; purchases outside top categories earn **1.0%**.
- Bronze Savings APY bonus: +0%; Gold Account APY bonus: +0.2%.
- A $500 signup statement-credit offer required account opening from 2025-01-01 through 2025-06-30 and $2,000 in posted purchases in the first month. It cannot be counted after that window, and low monthly spending may not meet the requirement.

### Gold Rewards Card

- Minimum credit score: **720**.
- Active Rho-Bank+ subscription: **required**.
- Annual fee: **$0**.
- Cash back on all purchases: **2.5%**.
- Gold Account minimum is reduced to $5,000 while the qualifying card is active and linked.
- Gold Account card APY bonus: +0.025%; Bronze Savings card APY bonus: +0.25%.
- The subscription price is not supplied. Include it as an unknown cost rather than assuming it is free.

### EcoCard

- No minimum credit-score requirement is stated.
- Annual fee: **$50**.
- Purchase APR: 19.99%; foreign transaction fee: 1.0%; late fee: $32.50.
- Signup offer requires $5,000 in eligible purchases within the first month for 2,000 sustainability points. Do not count it when the customer cannot meet the spending threshold.
- Rewards are sustainability points rather than cash-back labeling; one point redeems at $0.01, but category-specific earnings must be established before estimating rewards.
- EcoCard does not reduce the Gold Account minimum; consequently a customer with only $5,000 cannot rely on the Gold Account through this card alone.

### Diamond Elite Card

- Invitation only. A reported absence of an invitation makes it unavailable for recommendation as an open-now option.
- Consideration guidance names a score of at least 780, but meeting that guidance does not guarantee an invitation.

## Reward representation

Transaction records may label credit-card rewards as points. For cash-back cards, one stored point equals $0.01 when redeemed as a statement credit or checking credit. EcoCard sustainability points also redeem at $0.01 per point, but its earning categories/rates must still be known before projecting them.
