# Verified business-checking recommendation facts

Use these facts only for account-selection conversations covered by the supplied materials. A missing fact is unknown, not a favorable inference.

## November 2025 promotional ordering

From 2025-11-01 through 2025-11-30 inclusive, if multiple business checking accounts meet **all** stated customer requirements, recommend Sky Blue first and Lime Green second. Do not recommend either promotional account when it fails a customer requirement.

## Sky Blue

- Company must be within four years of formation.
- The startup has a six-month free period; afterward the monthly maintenance fee is $25.00.
- Overdraft fee is $0.00.

## Lime Green

- Monthly maintenance fee is $25.00.
- Maintenance fee is waived at a $15,000 balance as defined for the account; do not assume a customer will meet this threshold.
- End-of-day balance of at least $5,000 is described as the minimum balance requirement for benefits and to avoid balance-related charges.
- Overdraft fee is $0.00.
- It offers dedicated account-manager support, with direct-message response within two business hours and priority-line waits of up to six minutes.
- Outgoing domestic wires are $10.00 and outgoing international wires are $25.00. It includes up to 10 incoming domestic wires per monthly cycle at no charge.
- APY is 1.5% with daily compounding; daily mobile check deposit limit is $15,000 and total daily transaction capacity is $100,000.

## Cobalt Blue facts relevant to comparison

- Monthly maintenance fee is $20.00 and its stated waiver balance is $2,500.
- It does not assess overdraft fees.
- It earns 0.5% APY with daily compounding and has a $10,000 daily mobile-deposit limit.

These facts alone do not establish that Cobalt Blue provides any unlisted premium or enhanced-service feature. Do not treat it as the preferred option over an actively prioritized qualifying promotional account without a customer requirement that makes the promoted account unsuitable.

## Account-opening reminder

Opening a business checking account is separate from recommending one. The supplied opening procedure requires verified identity, at least one existing OPEN personal checking account, no more than six business checking accounts, no CLOSED accounts, and an existing checking-account balance of at least $500 before using the designated opening tool.
