All programs are subject to general referral restrictions. The referred customer must be new to Rho-Bank, with no existing checking, savings, or closed account within the prior 12 months; the parties cannot have the same registered address. For business referrals, the referred business's primary owner/authorized signer SSN must differ from the primary owner of any existing Rho-Bank business account. Qualifying deposits must be new money and retained for at least 30 days after the qualifying period. Bonuses can be clawed back for referred-account closure within 90 days and cannot be combined with another new-account promotion.

Across all checking products, a referrer may receive no more than two completed referral bonuses in a rolling nine-day window. The target-program annual limits above are separate program terms.
