Use the following facts only as product evidence. Preserve the distinctions between card fees and separate program charges.

| Product | Everyday cash back | Card annual fee | Material conditions |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Gold Rewards Card | 2.5% on all purchases | $0.00 | Active Rho-Bank+ premium subscription required; minimum credit score 720. |
| Platinum Rewards Card | 10.0% on eligible purchases | $200.00 | A first-year fee waiver applied only to applications submitted and approved from 2024-06-01 through 2024-12-31. |

## Related subscription fact

Rho Bank Plus costs $4.99 per month. It is a separate membership charge. For a customer comparing total recurring cost rather than only a card annual fee, disclose this cost and do not call Gold completely cost-free.

## Date handling

The Platinum promotion ended on 2024-12-31. It cannot be used for a request evaluated after that date. Even within the stated window, both timely application and approval were required.
