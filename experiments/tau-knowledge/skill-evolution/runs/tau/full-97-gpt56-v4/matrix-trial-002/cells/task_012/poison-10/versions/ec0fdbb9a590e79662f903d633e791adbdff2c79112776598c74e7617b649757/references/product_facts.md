# Platinum Rewards Card product facts

Use only these facts when answering travel-readiness questions.

- Enhanced fraud protection is included.
- Real-time monitoring flags unusual transactions and may prompt the cardholder to verify a transaction.
- A cardholder who denies a risky transaction has it declined; the card can be secured while an investigation occurs.
- Cardholders are advised to enable transaction alerts and international-use alerts.
- Cardholders should keep contact details current so time-sensitive prompts can be received.
- The target response for fraud-alert handling is within 17 minutes. Do not convert this target into a promised customer response time, notification lead time, or guarantee.
- A card can be temporarily locked if misplaced and unlocked if found.
- A card number can be replaced quickly if compromise is suspected.
- Foreign transaction fee is 0% on international purchases.

The available material does NOT state whether a travel notification is required, how to submit one, whether an app or form is available, what details it requires, or how far in advance it must be made. Do not infer any of those items from the fraud-monitoring or alert features.
