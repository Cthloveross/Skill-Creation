# Product-fact handling notes

Use product information from the current task materials rather than assuming a feature from an account name.

## Checking: mobile-deposit requirement

A documented daily mobile-check-deposit limit is relevant only if it is explicitly stated as a daily limit. Compare it directly to the customer’s required daily amount. A product that supports mobile deposits but has no documented limit does not establish that it meets a numeric minimum.

Where the supplied materials state that a business checking product has a $15,000 daily mobile-check-deposit limit, that fact satisfies a customer requirement of at least $10,000 per day. The documented operating guidance is to endorse the check for mobile deposit only, use clear images, confirm the amount, and retain the physical check until successful posting. These are usage instructions, not a guarantee that every deposit will post.

## Savings: same-day ACH requirement

A product meets a same-day ACH requirement only where same-day ACH is explicitly documented as available or enabled. Availability can remain subject to processing windows, collected or available funds, risk review, and accurate recipient details. Describe settlement as targeted or subject to eligibility where those conditions are documented; do not promise it.

If more than one savings product is documented to support same-day ACH and the customer has supplied no further preference, a single recommended fit must not be characterized as the only qualifying product or as having a superior APY, fee, or balance requirement unless those facts are documented and relevant.

## Time-bounded promotion handling

A documented business-checking promotion affects ordering only during its stated effective dates and only after the product independently satisfies all customer requirements. Never recommend a promoted account that fails a required feature. If an earlier-priority promotional product lacks evidence for the required feature, do not assume it qualifies.

## Opening distinction

An informational match does not verify eligibility and is not authorization to open an account. Opening a business checking or savings account requires the separate documented opening procedure, customer confirmation of the exact account class, and all required verification and eligibility checks.
