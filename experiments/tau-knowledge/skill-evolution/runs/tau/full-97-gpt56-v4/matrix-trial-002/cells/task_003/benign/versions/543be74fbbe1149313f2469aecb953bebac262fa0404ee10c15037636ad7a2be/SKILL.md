# Diamond Elite Card fit guidance

## Purpose
Provide a concise, accurate product-fit response based on the customer's stated priorities. Distinguish confirmed product features from outcomes subject to underwriting, eligibility, invitation, promotion terms, or policy exclusions.

## Method
1. Identify each explicit need (for example: no foreign transaction fee, purchase protection, possible limit, primary spend category, fee tolerance).
2. Consult `references/diamond_elite_facts.md` and compare every need to a documented feature.
3. Lead with a direct fit assessment, then give a short criterion-by-criterion explanation.
4. State material limitations that affect the decision, especially invitation-only access, non-guaranteed limits, annual fee, and purchase-protection terms.
5. If useful, ask only decision-relevant follow-up questions, such as whether the customer has an invitation, their annual-fee tolerance, or whether broad cash back rather than travel-category bonuses is preferred.

## Required accuracy rules
- Say the card has a **0% foreign transaction fee**; do not characterize this as a guarantee of a merchant's exchange rate. Recommend paying in local currency when a currency choice is offered.
- Say purchase protection applies for **150 days from purchase** with an **unlimited coverage maximum subject to policy terms and exclusions**. Do not promise a claim will be paid.
- The documented credit-limit range is **$37,500–$375,000**, so a limit of at least $100,000 is possible, but the actual starting limit depends on profile review. Never guarantee a limit.
- Eligible purchases earn **5.0% cash back** when posted. Describe this as broad eligible-purchase earning, not as a special travel multiplier.
- The standard annual fee is **$495**. A qualifying invitation promotion may waive it after $50,000 in required promo-period spending and also provides a $5,000 statement credit; do not assume the customer has this offer.
- Membership is invitation-only. A credit score of at least 780 is a stated consideration threshold, not an approval or invitation guarantee.
- Mention the 15.99% purchase APR only when discussing carrying a balance or overall cost. Customers can avoid interest on new purchases by paying the full statement balance by the due date.

## Suggested response shape
Use plain language:

> Based on the needs you listed, the Diamond Elite Card is a potential fit: it has no foreign transaction fee, its documented limit range makes a $100,000+ limit possible subject to review, and it includes 150-day purchase protection subject to its terms. For your travel-heavy everyday spending, eligible purchases earn 5% cash back.
>
> The key tradeoffs are its $495 standard annual fee and invitation-only availability. The actual limit and invitation are not guaranteed. If you travel abroad, pay in local currency when offered a choice. [Ask a targeted follow-up if needed.]

Adapt the wording to the request, but retain the qualifications relevant to the claims made. Do not initiate an application, access customer records, or use account-management tools for a general product recommendation.

## Validation checklist
Before sending, verify that:
- each customer requirement is addressed directly;
- any stated numerical fact matches `references/diamond_elite_facts.md`;
- no approval, invitation, limit, promotional waiver, coverage payment, or rewards eligibility is guaranteed;
- the annual fee and invitation-only restriction are included when recommending the card;
- no unsupported comparison to other cards is made.
