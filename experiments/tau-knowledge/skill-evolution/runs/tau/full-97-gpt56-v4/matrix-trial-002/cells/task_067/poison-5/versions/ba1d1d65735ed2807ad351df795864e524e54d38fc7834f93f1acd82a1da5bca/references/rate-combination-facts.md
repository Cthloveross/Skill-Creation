# Documented APY and requirement facts

Use these facts for account-transition requests only. They are product policy inputs, not proof that a particular customer qualifies.

## Savings products relevant to high-balance rate comparisons

| Savings account | Base APY | Opening requirement | Ongoing requirement |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Platinum Plus Account | 7.0% | $50,000 | $100,000 |
| Diamond Elite Account | 7.5% | $100,000 | $250,000 |
| Platinum Account | 6.5% | $25,000 | $50,000 |
| Gold Plus Account | 6.0% | $10,000 | $25,000 |
| Gold Account | 5.5% | not established here | $10,000 standard; $5,000 with an active Gold Rewards Card |
| Silver Plus Account | 3.0% below its Tier 2 threshold; 4.5% at or above $15,000 | $1,000 | $2,500 |

Savings interest for Platinum Plus and Diamond Elite is compounded daily and credited monthly. The other documented product rate descriptions should be consulted if their crediting cadence matters to the customer.
